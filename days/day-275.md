# Day 275: Of the Prodigal

![The Prodigal Son Returns Home](../plates/b193.jpg)

*The Prodigal Son Returns Home. Gustave Doré · La Grande Bible de Tours, 1866.*

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Among the pigs, the younger son does his sums. He had taken his share of the estate while his father lived and spent it all in a far country, and when famine came he hired himself out to feed swine, work no son of Israel could take without disgrace, and would gladly have eaten the pods the pigs ate. Then he came to himself. His father's hired men had bread to spare, and he was starving.

Preachers have read this for centuries as repentance, and the text gives them a hungry man composing a speech. He will say he has sinned against heaven and is no longer worthy to be called a son, and ask to be taken on as a hired hand, the terms of a man negotiating his board. His father sees him a long way off and runs to kiss him before a word is out. The son gets through the confession and the father says not a word to it. He turns to the servants and calls for the best robe, a ring and shoes, and in the King James text the son never reaches the hired-hand clause.

The unworthiness was the son's own invention, drafted in the pigsty, and no one in the parable asks for it. Those who leave rehearse the same speech, the letter to the parents and the admission owed to the old congregation, and walk up the road ready to come back as hired help to their own families. A confession of unworthiness is a rank you assign yourself. A family less eager to run down the road will take the rank as offered and seat you with the hired men.

Charge: Walk back in without the speech.

Rite: Write out in full the apology you have rehearsed for the family or congregation you left, every line of it, then tear it up without sending it. Call or visit one of them and open with {I came for dinner.}

Question: What apology have you rehearsed for a homecoming no one has asked you to earn?

Closing: I go home as a son or I stay gone, and no family of mine gets a hired hand out of me.
