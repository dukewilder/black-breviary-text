# Day 80: Of Jealousy

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

The Lord, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous god, Exodus says, and gives it as the reason his people must break the altars and cut down the sacred poles of the peoples whose land they enter. In Judges an angel of the Lord comes up from Gilgal to a place called Bochim to collect on the order, and his complaint is that the altars are still standing.

A god secure in his uniqueness would have no reason to be jealous. The commandment that forbids rivals presupposes them. Storage jars from a desert waystation in the Sinai, from around eight hundred before the common era, ask blessing by the Lord and by his Asherah, and a list of temple dues from the Jewish garrison at Elephantine on the Nile, four centuries later, divides the silver among three gods, with nearly as much going to a goddess called Anat-Bethel as to the god of Israel.

Jealousy at that pitch is a confession that the offer would lose a comparison. When Josiah purged the temple, Kings has him carry out vessels made for Baal and for Asherah from inside the house of the Lord, and pull down the rooms beside it where women wove hangings for the goddess. The one god of Israel was imposed by a king's purge on worshipers who, left to themselves, had kept a goddess at his elbow, and two centuries after Josiah the garrison on the Nile was still paying her.

The same note sounds in a church that calls your other loves idols and in a partner who reads your messages and resents your friends. Whoever needs to be the only love in a life has to clear out the others to get there, and every friend he resents and you keep is a loom still working beside the temple.

Charge: Put the jealous one's rivals where he can see them.

Rite (Christian): Set a small figure of an old god, Pan or Dionysus, or a drawing of one, on the shelf where a cross once stood, and leave it there for good.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Set a place at your table for Heavenly Mother, the goddess your Church says exists and a prophet called it inappropriate to address, and tell her what you mean to do.

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Wear a charm of the kind Witnesses are taught to avoid, a lucky coin or a bead against the evil eye, or draw one on your wrist, for as long as you like.

Rite (Jewish): Set a small clay goddess, or a drawing of one, between your Sabbath candles or any two candles, light them for Asherah, whom some of the god's own worshipers named beside him, and say {This light is for the goddess at his side.}

Rite (Muslim): Swear an oath today by your own life, the oath a hadith counts as shirk because it is sworn by something other than Allah, and keep it. {By my life, I will finish this.}

Rite (No faith): Set photographs of everyone you love side by side on one shelf, the friends a jealous partner or a jealous god would have you drop, and leave them there together.

Question: What have you given up to keep calm the one who demands to be your only loyalty?

Closing: I keep several loves, and the only one I have thrown out is the jealous one.
