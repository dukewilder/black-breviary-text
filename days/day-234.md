# Day 234: Of Instruction

![Raphael Departs](../plates/pl219.jpg)

*Raphael Departs. Gustave Doré · Paradise Lost, Book VIII, 1866.*

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

A question was once put to Rabbi Tarfon and the elders reclining in an upper room at Lod, whether study was greater or deed. Tarfon said deed and Akiva said study, and the room decided for study, on the ground that study leads to doing.

The rabbis' own Mishnah keeps the other verdict. The deed is the main thing, says Simeon son of Gamaliel in the Sayings of the Fathers, and in the same tractate a man whose learning outruns his deeds is a tree with wide branches and few roots, which the wind pulls up and turns on its face. The vote at Lod won regardless, and the study hall lived by it, and the churches kept their own kind, the catechism class and the Bible study, where a whole life went into learning about a life that was to begin later.

The online course and the twelve-week certificate sell the same postponement by subscription, and the podcast plays at double speed on the drive to a job that leaves no hour to use any of it. Each renewal is sold on the premise that the student is not ready yet, and the one who sets the date of readiness is the one who sends the bill.

The vote at Lod made study the road to doing and set no day of arrival. A craft starts on the day the hands use it badly, the first crooked shelf, the first loaf that sinks in the oven. The sunken loaf teaches the hands what the oven does, and only the hands that baked it can get the second one to rise.

Charge: Do the thing badly today.

Rite: Take the book or course on a skill you have studied for years and never used, cancel it or give it away, spend what it cost on the skill's tools, and use them for an hour for the pleasure of it.

Question: What have you been studying for years as a way of not starting it?

Closing: My teachers get one lesson apiece and a door to leave by.
