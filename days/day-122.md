# Day 122: Of Judgment

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Solomon called for a sword to settle a case that had no witnesses. Two prostitutes shared a house, and the woman who brought the case said that nobody else was in it on the night one of them lay on her own baby and smothered it, so that each now said the living boy was hers and no one could contradict either. The king ordered the child cut in two, half to each woman. The real mother begged him to give the boy to her rival and let him live, and got him back.

Every fact in the case was known before the sword came out, and known to the women. Solomon supplied a threat. The mother supplied the truth, and the chapter closes by crediting the wisdom of the god, which all Israel saw in the king, and they feared him. In the version Bukhari preserves, where a wolf carries off one woman's son and David first rules for the elder woman, Solomon calls for a knife, and the knowledge again comes out of the mother's mouth.

Advice on fact is worth buying, a lawyer reading over a lease or a surgeon reading a scan, because the facts sit with someone else. On what you want, the case has one witness, as the case in Solomon's house had only the two women to testify, and the witness is you. A counselor who calls for a sword is asking you to say aloud what you hold, so say it first and spare him the blade.

Charge: Buy facts from experts and decide the rest yourself.

Rite: Write the decision you keep taking to a pastor, parent, imam or coach as one sentence beginning I have decided, sign it, and send it to that person today, asking nothing back. Then say aloud {The case is closed.}

Question: Which answer did you already have the last time you asked someone for advice?

Closing: The only witness to what I want is me, and I will not hand my verdict to any adviser.
