# Day 293: Of the Thief

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Only Luke has a penitent thief. In Mark and Matthew both men crucified beside him join in the abuse, but Luke gives one of them a rebuke for his fellow (they deserve their sentence, he says, and this man does not) and a request to be remembered when Jesus comes into his kingdom, and gives Jesus the reply that the man will be with him in paradise that same day. Later tradition named him Dismas and made him a saint, patron of prisoners.

Preachers kept a story like Luke's close at hand. In 1915 an evangelist named Lady Hope told a Bible conference at Northfield, Massachusetts, that Darwin on his sickbed had been reading the Epistle to the Hebrews and wished aloud that he had not put his theory as he did. The story ran in a Baptist paper that summer and went round in tracts for decades. Darwin's daughter Henrietta answered in 1922 that he never recanted any of his scientific views.

A deathbed is a scene where the dying man cannot answer back, and that is what made Darwin's so useful to the tract. Yours is as open to the same use if pain and morphine take your grip and a visitor writes down the old words coming back.

Darwin, who could not answer, had a daughter who could. Henrietta had been at his deathbed, and Lady Hope, she wrote, had been present at no illness of his at all. A conclusion signed while the head is clear leaves a witness the preacher cannot out-talk.

Charge: Decline the deathbed bargain in advance.

Rite (Christian): Cut the thief's promise of paradise out of your own Bible, write I decline on the back, say {I decline} aloud, and file the strip with your important papers.

Rite (Catholic): Print the Hail Mary and cut away the words now and at the hour of our death. Read the shortened prayer aloud, then slip the strip into a novel you love as a bookmark.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): In your own Book of Mormon, strike a line through Alma's warning against putting off repentance until the end, and write beside it that repentance is owed to no one, now or at the end. Sign it.

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): In your own New World Translation, black out Jesus' promise of paradise to the thief, write Not today and not later over it, and say {I will not be in your paradise, today or later.}

Rite (No faith): Write on a card that no chaplain, priest or prayer is wanted at your death and that nothing you say at the end retracts what you concluded before it. Sign it, read it aloud, and give it to whoever will be with you.

Rite (Jewish): Cut the deathbed confession out of your own prayer book, or a printed copy, tape the strip inside a cupboard door, and say aloud the last words you would choose in its place.

Rite (Muslim): Write on a card that no one is to prompt you with the testimony of faith as you die, sign it, read it aloud, and tape it inside the cover of your own Quran.

Question: What do you want on record in place of the words you fear you will say at the end?

Closing: A paradise that waits for the last sentence of a life has no use for the thousand days before it, and I owe it nothing.
