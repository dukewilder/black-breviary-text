# Day 270: Of Seizures

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Jesus's disciples failed to cure a boy whose father had brought him to them. Moonstruck is Matthew's word for him, and Mark describes the fits well enough for any neurologist to name them, the boy thrown down, foaming, grinding his teeth and going rigid. The father is told that everything is possible for one who believes, and cries that he believes and begs for help with his unbelief. Afterward the disciples ask privately why they could not do it, and are told this kind goes out only by prayer, to which later manuscripts add fasting, a word the oldest copies lack.

Nearly five centuries before the gospels, a Greek medical treatise argued that the sacred disease was no more sacred than any other and had a natural cause like the rest. Many churches kept the demon for centuries after. In nineteen seventy-five a young German woman with a diagnosis of epilepsy was given the full rite of exorcism on a bishop's word, and she went on kneeling and fasting through it, the fasting a later scribe had written into Mark. She died the next year, at twenty-three, of starvation and dehydration, and in nineteen seventy-eight her parents and the two priests were convicted of negligent homicide.

A seizure is a body doing what bodies do, and the answer to it is a clock and a pair of trained hands, a skill learned in ten minutes. The trained bystander times it, clears the space, cushions the head, holds nobody down, puts nothing in the mouth, turns the person onto one side once the jerking stops and stays beside them, and calls for emergency help past five minutes or if they do not wake. The two priests knew their rite to the word, and they read it over her some sixty times while she starved.

Charge: Know what the exorcists did not, and command the room.

Rite: Read the Epilepsy Foundation's guide to seizure first aid, then give every order aloud, from the clock to the cushion to the turn onto the side, to an adult who agrees to lie on the floor, or to a cushion if you are alone.

Question: Whom would you most want beside you on a floor, with trained hands?

Closing: When someone seizes on my floor, I reach for the clock, and every prayer book in the house stays shut.
