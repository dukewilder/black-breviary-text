# Day 230: Of Increase

![The Creation of the Creatures](../plates/pl207.jpg)

*The Creation of the Creatures. Gustave Doré · Paradise Lost, Book VII, 1866.*

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

The first blessing in the Bible is spoken to fish and birds. On the fifth day the god makes the great sea creatures and everything that swarms in the water and every winged bird, and blesses them, and tells them to be fruitful and multiply and fill the seas. The beasts of the land come the next day with no blessing of their own, and humans after them hear the same words the fish heard, with dominion added. The Catholic rite of marriage still offers that passage as a first reading over bride and groom.

The blessing hardened into policy wherever an institution counted on its members. Rome declared in nineteen sixty-eight that every marital act must stay open to life. Latter-day Saint leaders declared in nineteen ninety-five that the commandment to multiply and replenish the earth remains in force, on the teaching that spirit children in a premortal existence accepted a plan to obtain bodies. A member born into the pews needs no missionary, and a church that counts its future in baptisms has its own reasons to call your fertility a calling.

A child lives the whole of a life someone else chose to begin, and a commandment is the poorest reason ever offered for beginning one. The grandmother who wants a christening photograph and the pastor with a building fund will be asleep at three in the morning when the fever comes. Wanting the long work of raising a person is a reason, and so is wanting your money and your nights for yourself, and the choice between them belongs to whoever will be holding the thermometer.

Charge: Have children for your reasons, or not at all.

Rite: Send the person who presses you hardest about children, a parent, an in-law or a pastor, one message and add nothing to it. {Whether I have children is mine to decide, and the subject is closed.} Then spend what a month of diapers would cost on something for yourself alone.

Question: Whose voice speaks first when you think about having children?

Closing: A blessing the god spoke first to the fish has no say over my body.
