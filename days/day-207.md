# Day 207: Of the Field

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Revelation gives Michael the victory in heaven and then shows what the winners did with it. Michael's host casts the dragon and all his followers down to earth, a loud voice calls on the heavens to rejoice, and the same voice adds a woe for everyone living below, since the devil has come down to them in a great rage. Heaven kept its feast and left the loser loose on the ground underneath, where within a few verses he is hunting the woman into the wilderness.

The first book of Maccabees records a better soldier. After Judas retook Jerusalem and purified the temple, he spent his next effort on masonry, raising high walls and strong towers around Mount Zion, setting a garrison in them and fortifying Beth-zur as a stronghold facing Idumea. The book gives the work two verses. Beth-zur, short of food in a fallow year, surrendered to the boy king Antiochus, but Mount Zion stood a siege until his regent, hearing that another general had come back to seize the government, told him their food was short and the place was strong, and the king offered the defenders terms.

Whatever you won was won from somebody, the post another wanted or the client who used to be a rival's, and the one who lost it studies your ground the way the dispossessed always do, looking for the low place. The winner who celebrates and builds nothing has done what heaven did, thrown the loser just far enough to come back angry. Antiochus went in under the peace, saw the strength of the place, broke his oath and had the wall around it pulled down. Of everything Judas had won, the wall was the one thing his enemy would perjure himself to be rid of.

Charge: Fortify what you won before you celebrate it.

Rite: Name aloud the rival who wants back what you took from them. Then close today the one gap they would use, the password or the lock changed, the deal signed in your own name, their access ended.

Question: Where would the rival you beat come over the wall?

Closing: Heaven threw its dragon down onto the ground I live on and went off singing, so I do my own masonry.
