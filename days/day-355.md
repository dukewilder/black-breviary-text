# Day 355: Of Lucifer

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Over its great candle at the Easter Vigil the Roman rite gives Christ the name Lucifer, the morning star that never sets. The taunt asks how the son of the morning is fallen from heaven. The word is Latin for the bringer of light. The Latin Bible put the same word into Isaiah's taunt, and Origen and others read it as the devil's name.

The plate gives the name to a figure frozen in ice to the middle of his chest, great bat's wings spread behind him and his chin resting on his clasped hands. He does not speak in the plate, and in the book it illustrates he weeps as he chews and says nothing. The older books let him talk, and even the Gospels let him speak in the wilderness. In Job the satan is asked where he has come from and answers directly, with a report of his travels over the earth. The satan of Job stood in the divine council and disputed with the god over Job, and the Quran's Iblis answered his maker to his face, asked for a reprieve until the day of resurrection, and was granted it.

The church kept the name's light for its own Christ, and a later pen gave its bearer the ice and the silence. A household can hand the same arrangement to whoever leaves. That silence was written in the fourteenth century, long after Job and the Quran let him argue, and it holds only while the one in the ice agrees to keep it.

Charge: Speak up at the table that expects your silence.

Rite (Christian): Copy the verse in your own Bible where Jesus calls himself the bright morning star onto a slip, with Lucifer written over the name of Jesus, paste it over the printed verse, and read it aloud to whoever is in the room, or to the empty room.

Rite (Catholic): Copy the line of the great candle hymn that hails Christ as the morning star who knows no setting, from a missal or a printout, writing Lucifer wherever Christ is named in it, and sing it aloud from your page.

Rite (Protestant): Read Isaiah's taunt over the son of the morning aloud from your own Bible, copy it out with each mocking line turned to praise, glue the sheet over the page, and say {He did not fall.}

Rite (Orthodox): Copy the evening hymn O Gladsome Light onto a page with the name Lucifer wherever Christ is addressed, light a lamp or a candle, the lamp before your icons if you keep one, and sing or say the page by its flame.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Copy out Lucifer's offer at the council in heaven, that not one soul would be lost, in your own hand, paste it over the Beloved Son's answer in your own Pearl of Great Price, and read it aloud as your own argument.

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Find the words bright morning star in the last chapter of Revelation in your own Bible, or on a page you print, write Lucifer over them, and read the line aloud as it now stands.

Rite (Jewish): Print Isaiah's taunt over the shining one, or use the page in your own Tanakh, write risen across every word that says fallen or cast down, and read it aloud standing up.

Rite (Muslim): Copy out the two verses in which Iblis asks for respite until the day of resurrection and is granted it, with your own name in place of his, paste the slip over them in your own Quran, and read it aloud. Alone, with the door shut.

Rite (No faith): Write on a card the one thing about your leaving you have never said at a family table, and say it aloud now, to whoever is with you, or over your own plate where saying it to them would bring danger.

Question: Where have you caught yourself posing as the silent one who left?

Closing: The ice was only ever ink, and I have a voice that pen never gave him.
