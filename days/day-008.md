# Day 8: Of Nakedness

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Noah plants a vineyard and lies drunk and uncovered in his tent. Ham sees him there and goes out to tell his brothers, and the brothers come in backward with a garment on their shoulders and cover their father without looking at him. When Noah wakes and learns what was done, he curses Ham's son Canaan instead. Two rabbis in the Talmud disagreed on whether Ham castrated his father or raped him, and each read a deed into the verse that says Noah knew what his younger son had done to him, in a chapter that shows Ham only looking. Christian preachers kept the sentence and lost the grandson, so that by the nineteenth century the curse of Ham was being read from American pulpits as a title deed to African slaves, and the Latter-day Saints barred men of African descent from their priesthood until nineteen seventy-eight on reasoning drawn in part from the same lineage.

Shame falls on whoever was in the room and travels down, and a family keeps its patriarch's dignity by spending its children's. A child who found a parent drunk on the floor, or heard what should have stayed behind a door, learns that seeing was the crime, and spends years walking backward with a cloak, covering for the people who did the thing.

Guilt belongs to whoever did a specific harm, and it is settled in private, by changing course. Shame carried for another person's act has no course to change, and in Genesis the one who passed it down was Noah, to a grandson who was not in the tent.

Charge: Hand back every shame that was never yours.

Rite (Christian): Walk backward into a room with a coat on your shoulders, as two of Noah's sons did. Let it fall, turn, name the one whose act you covered, and refuse them the forgiveness the Lord's Prayer demands. {I see it. No pardon.}

Rite (Protestant): Take the purity ring or pledge card you were given, or draw one, declare the vow void aloud, and say whose shame it really was. Then drop the ring among your loose change, or the card in the recycling.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): If you were endowed, put one set of temple garments in the trash uncut, or anything you wore for modesty's sake if you were not, and hand the shame back aloud. {That shame was never mine.}

Rite (Jewish): Recite the Shema with nothing on, which the law forbids in the presence of uncovered flesh, keeping every word as you learned it. Then hand the shame back aloud. {That shame was never mine.}

Rite (Muslim): Take your own Quran in hand with nothing on and without first washing as for prayer, and read aloud from it slowly, the purity the jurists require ignored. Then hand the shame back. {That shame was never mine.}

Rite (No faith): Print Noah's curse on his grandson with the blessing of the god of Shem that follows it, read both aloud, and curse that god by name for letting it stand, the blasphemy Leviticus punishes with stoning. Then throw the page out.

Question: Whose act are you still covering for, and what has the covering cost you?

Closing: I side with the son who saw his father naked and told his brothers, and what I saw in my own house I will tell.
