# Day 314: Of the Pale Horse

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Of the four riders in Revelation only the fourth was given a name, and the name was Death. His horse was the green-gray of new shoots or of a corpse, a color English settled into pale. Hades followed with him. The two were given power over a fourth part of the earth, to kill with the sword and with famine and with plague and by the wild beasts.

Behind them the preachers marched a retinue gathered from later chapters and other books, everything they would sell as protection, the books opened before the white throne, Matthew's judgment with its sorting of sheep from goats and its fire kept for the goats. The sermon on the last things has run as a transaction ever since, terror of the rider exchanged for a ticket out of his company. Take that retinue away and Death rides as his own verse has him, with only the grave at his heels, and behind him comes the end of one particular life and the earth going on with no court sitting anywhere.

Death took the voice that told the same story at every dinner and got the ending wrong, and the hand that knew the bread was done by the sound of the crust. What he left behind was a chair still turned toward the window and a table one place short.

Without the fear they were hired to sell, what remains is the reluctance worth having, the measure of a life that was enjoyed, and it belongs to the last hour of a good party, when the music has stopped and somebody is bringing down the coats.

Charge: Live hard enough to dread leaving.

Rite (Christian): Recite the Apostles' Creed aloud backwards, word by word, from the life everlasting to God the Father almighty, as the black masses of legend did. Then say {One life, and I am spending it.}

Rite (Catholic): Write and sign a clause for your will ordering your body cremated, the ashes scattered at sea or on open ground, and no funeral Mass, the scattering Rome forbade in 2016. Read it aloud and file it with your papers.

Rite (Protestant): Cut out of your own Bible the verse that after death comes the judgment, burn it in a dish, and stir the ash into the soil of a house plant, saying {I came from dust, and I go back to it with no judgment waiting.}

Rite (Orthodox): Boil koliva, the wheat and honey of the memorial service, for your own memorial while you are alive, write your name across it in sugar, and eat the whole bowl yourself, saying {Memory eternal, and I was here for it.}

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Write and sign a clause for your will forbidding anyone to perform temple work for you after you die, baptism for the dead included. Read it aloud, {No one is to baptize me after I am dead}, and file it with your papers.

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Write your funeral wishes, a burial under a cross, a clergyman of Christendom to read over you and a church hymn at the grave, every one of them what the Society calls Babylon the Great. Sign it and read it aloud.

Rite (Jewish): Write and sign a statement that you want your body cremated, which Orthodox Jewish law forbids. Read aloud the waking blessing that praises the god who restores souls to dead bodies, answer it {No one will restore mine}, and tape the statement to the refrigerator.

Rite (Muslim): Wrap yourself in a white sheet as the dead are shrouded and lie down with your back to Mecca. Answer the question the hadith gives the angels of the grave, Who is your lord, with {I am my own lord.} Then throw off the sheet. Keep it private.

Rite (No faith): Plan your own wake, the menu and the music, with a signed clause barring every prayer and scripture from it. Then cook and eat its first course today at a table laid for two, the second place set for God and left empty.

Question: What would you most hate to leave if death came for you today, and when did you last enjoy it?

Closing: Death can have the body once I have worn it out, and the preachers get nothing.
