# Day 199: Of Fraud

![Geryon](../plates/inf121.jpg)

*Geryon. Gustave Doré · Inferno, Canto XVII, 1861.*

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Geryon, the beast of fraud coiled across the plate, has the face of a just man, kindly to look at, over the body of a serpent with a scorpion's sting in its tail. In nineteen eighty-six the faith healer Peter Popoff was showing the face. He called out the names and ailments of strangers in his audiences as though heaven had told him, and the skeptic James Randi and Alexander Jason, with a radio scanner, recorded the source, his wife backstage reading prayer cards into a receiver in his ear. The recording was played on The Tonight Show in May of that year, on national television, and his wife Elizabeth was the voice in the receiver.

After the broadcast his ratings and donations fell. He declared bankruptcy the next year, in nineteen eighty-seven, owing more than seven hundred creditors, was buying airtime again by the late nineties, and within a few more years was selling miracle spring water on late-night television in five countries, the United States and Canada among them. On 11 March 2025 the British regulator Ofcom fined the Word Network 150,000 pounds for carrying his claims.

By two thousand five, nearly twenty years after the tape, his ministry was taking in more than twenty-three million dollars a year. The tape that exposed him ran on a talk show, and the trick needs only an audience that never heard the wire or saw the tape.

Whoever has heard the tape, the wife's voice reading into the receiver, cannot be preached back out of what the ears heard.

Charge: Ask every miracle where the wire runs.

Rite: Watch a faith healer's broadcast with a friend who agrees to the game, and stop at each cure to name the trick aloud before he finishes, the prayer card filled out at the door, the name read back as a miracle.

Question: Which wonder you were once shown has had its wire explained to you, and what did you do with the envelope?

Closing: I give the faith healer the laughter of the room he robbed.
