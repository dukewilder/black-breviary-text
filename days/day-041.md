# Day 41: Of the Ass

![An Angel Appears to Balaam](../plates/b045.jpg)

*An Angel Appears to Balaam. Gustave Doré · La Grande Bible de Tours, 1866.*

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Scripture first gives the Hebrew word satan, adversary, to an angel of the god, posted in a road with a drawn sword to stop a man the god had just told to travel. Balaam, a seer hired by the king of Moab to curse Israel, had asked leave, received it and set out, and the divine anger was kindled because he went. The ass saw the angel.

Three times she balked, once crushing his foot against a wall, and three times he beat her, the third time with his staff, and then she spoke. The rabbis counted the mouth of the ass among the ten things made at twilight on the eve of the first Sabbath, so that it waited from the week of creation for one conversation. She asked whether in all the years he had ridden her she had ever behaved this way before. His eyes were opened, and the angel told him that if she had not turned aside he would now be dead and she alive.

She lays her years of carrying him against the one day she stopped and he has to admit she never balked before. Balaam was the seer who would soon call himself the man whose eyes are open, and the animal under him saw first.

A long record is evidence, owed a hearing before the stick. When a friend or a tradesman who has carried you for years suddenly balks, the years say something stands in the road, and whoever beats them for stopping beats the only one who saw it. A man who stalls every week with an excuse ready has built no such record, and the next job goes to a stranger who answers the telephone.

Charge: Ask what they saw before you punish the stop.

Rite: Of two people who kept you waiting lately, a friend, repairer or colleague, call the one with years of good work behind them today, ask what held them up, and pay what you owe. Give the other's next job or favor to someone else.

Question: Whose record with you has earned a hearing, and whose has run out?

Closing: I trust the ass that balked sooner than the prophet who beat her.
