# Day 361: Of Resolve

![Satan on Mount Niphates](../plates/pl110.jpg)

*Satan on Mount Niphates. Gustave Doré · Paradise Lost, Book IV, 1866.*

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Peter made his vow with the other disciples listening. All of them might fall away from Jesus that night, he said, but he never would, and when Jesus told him he would deny him three times before the cock crowed, Peter swore he would die with him first. The others said the same. Before morning, sitting with the servants by a fire in the high priest's courtyard, he was told by a serving girl, then by another, then by the bystanders, that he had been with the Galilean. He denied it, then denied it with an oath, and the third time began to curse and swear that he did not know the man. At once the cock crew, as Jesus had said.

The vow broke in front of a different audience from the one it was made to. At the fire the boast met servants and strangers, and a vow made for the twelve said nothing to them.

The Church builds resolution into penance. Absolution asks for contrition, and contrition includes a firm purpose of amendment, the resolution not to sin again. The same Church recommends confessing everyday faults regularly, which expects the faults back. Canon law says the same of the one who comes for absolution, that he must reject the sins committed and have a purpose of amendment.

A resolve made for an audience dies when the audience leaves the room. One that answers to the want alone fails on a Tuesday and starts again on Wednesday, because no one was ever owed its success, and no one is there to absolve it.

Charge: Take your vows off the stage.

Rite: Write on a card one resolution for something you want, small enough to keep this week and exact enough to break. Tell no one. Say over it {This one is for me}, put it in your wallet, and do the first piece today.

Question: Which of your resolutions was made for someone watching?

Closing: I break my resolutions in private and keep them the same way, and no confessor takes a share of either.
