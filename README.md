# The Black Breviary

*A Daily Devotion to the Self*

The Black Breviary is a free daily Satanic devotional, a book of hours for those who have left a faith or never had one. It runs 365 days. Each day brings one engraving by Gustave Doré or another public-domain artist, a reading, a charge, a rite written in the forms of the faith the reader left, a question for the reader's own pages and a declaration. The Satan of the book is the figure from the oldest story, who asked a question, offered the fruit and would not kneel, and here he stands for the self. The book asks for no belief in any devil, as it asks obedience of no god.

This repository holds the complete text, dedicated to the public domain under CC0.

## Files

- [`The-Black-Breviary.txt`](The-Black-Breviary.txt): the full text of the print book in reading order (about 217,000 words): the front matter, all 365 days, the feasts, the rites for every faith and the sources. Footnotes are shown as [1]; there are no page numbers.
- [`days/`](days/): each of the 365 days as its own Markdown file, from `day-001.md` (Of Waking) to `day-365.md` (Of the Stars). Days 1 to 121 are Book I, Burden; days 122 to 243 are Book II, Refusal; days 244 to 365 are Book III, Assent.
