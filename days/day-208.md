# Day 208: Of Hooks

![The Devils with Hooks](../plates/inf141.jpg)

*The Devils with Hooks. Gustave Doré · Inferno, Canto XXI, 1861.*

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Baptism, by Catholic doctrine, prints an indelible mark on the soul that no sin can erase. From nineteen eighty-three canon law recognized a formal act by which a Catholic could defect from the Church, and three marriage canons released whoever made it from rules that still bound every other baptized Catholic. In two thousand nine the Vatican struck those clauses out, so that the formal act no longer released anyone from any of them, and the mark stayed where doctrine had put it.

Jesus called his first disciples off their boats with a promise to make them fishers of men, and pulpits read the line as a mercy. Jeremiah had already used the figure, as a punishment. I will send for many fishers, says his god, and they shall fish them, and after them many hunters to hunt them out of the holes of the rocks, because he means to repay their sin double. Habakkuk complained that the conqueror hauls up whole nations with the hook and the net and then burns incense to the net.

The hooks are softer now and better aimed. The ministering visit, the verse texted on a birthday, a grandmother's tears, the elder who says the congregation misses you, each comes baited with something you really do miss, and the love in the bait is real. Rome's mark has no line on it. The lines run from the birthday and the funeral, and whoever knows where the barb sits can go to both, eat the cake, and leave without a promise.

Charge: Refuse the hook, however kind the hand on the pole.

Rite (Christian): Write on a card {I love you, and I am not coming back.} Tape a fishhook beside the words, keep the card by your phone for the next person who asks you back to church, and read it aloud once tonight.

Rite (Catholic): Take off the scapular or medal you were given to wear, thread it onto a fishhook as bait, drop it in the trash, and say {That is bait, and I am not biting.}

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Paint over a picture of the temple, your own or one you print, until no spire shows. Write across it the one sentence you will give every missionary or bishop who calls, and hang it from a fishhook inside your front door.

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Hang your own Watchtower, or any invitation you were handed, from a fishhook inside your front door and say {I take the love and leave the hook.}

Rite (Jewish): Hang your own kippah, or any cap you own, on a hook inside the bathroom door and dry your hands on it today.

Rite (Muslim): Hang your prayer beads on a fishhook from a nail by the front door. When the next prayer time comes, walk past them and out of the house, and say {The beads stay on the hook, and I go out.}

Rite (No faith): Drive a fishhook through the middle of a gospel tract you were handed, or the printed text of any tract, and pin it to your wall by the hook.

Question: What will you say, once and in advance, to the hook from the old faith that still catches you?

Closing: Rome can keep its indelible mark, since it was printed on a soul I never had.
