# Day 327: Of the Prophet Split

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Whoever wishes to be saved, the Athanasian Creed begins, must before all things hold the Catholic faith, and whoever does not keep it whole and undefiled will without doubt perish everlastingly. Clergy chanted it at Prime and Anglican congregations recited it on feast days, as a creed, for centuries. Christian polemic had long filed Islam as a heresy and its prophet as a renegade, and the engraving above shows the prophet of Islam in a Christian hell, split from chin to groin.

The tractate Gittin, in the William Davidson edition of the Talmud, tells of Onkelos raising Yeshu the Nazarene from the dead by necromancy to ask his sentence, and hearing it, boiling excrement, for having mocked the words of the sages. The Quran honors Jesus as a prophet and promises the fire to those who associate others with the god, and counts the claim that the god is the Messiah, son of Mary, as one. The Book of Mormon repeats the line from the end of Mark, that whoever believes not shall be damned.

Add the sentences together and nearly every human being who ever lived is in somebody's hell. Jesus is in one of them. Read side by side, the damnations look like warrants issued by rival courts, each enforceable only where its court sits and void everywhere else, and no court has jurisdiction over a defendant who walked out of all of them. That defendant stands in the largest crowd in history, and the sentences passed on the people you love have no officer to serve them.

Charge: Count yourself among the damned, and sleep soundly.

Rite (Christian): Copy the verse that says whoever does not believe is condemned already onto rice paper or an unconsecrated wafer with an edible marker, spread it with honey as Ezekiel's scroll was sweet in his mouth, and eat it. Say {Condemned, and content.}

Rite (Catholic): Write the Athanasian Creed's sentence, that whoever does not keep the Catholic faith whole will perish everlastingly, across any sheet of paper. Lay an unconsecrated wafer or a plain cracker on it and burn both in a sink.

Rite (Protestant): Burn the pages of your own Bible that describe the lake of fire in a grill or outdoor bowl, reading each aloud before it goes in, and roast marshmallows over the flames.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Alone, with the light off, say aloud the knowing denial of the Holy Ghost that the Book of Mormon calls unpardonable, {I deny the Holy Ghost, and I know what I am doing.} Then name each loved one that a creed damns and say {In good company.}

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Print the page of a Watchtower publication that foretells destruction at Armageddon, strike its heading through, burn it in a sink, and say {In good company.} over the ash.

Rite (Jewish): Find the Talmud page that tells of a mocker of the sages judged by boiling excrement, and print it. Take it into the lavatory, where the law forbids holy words, read it aloud there, and mock the sages aloud for a full minute.

Rite (Muslim): Print the verse that promises the fire to whoever calls God the Messiah, son of Mary. Behind a closed door, say aloud toward Mecca {God is the Messiah, son of Mary.} Then fold the page and sit on it through a full meal.

Rite (No faith): Find the verse near the end of Mark that damns whoever does not believe, and print it. Say the names of three people you love, each followed by {In good company}, then bury the page in a flowerpot and plant something that fruits over it.

Question: Which of your old faith's damnations did you believe longest?

Closing: The fires disagree about who burns, and I leave them to settle the matter between themselves.
