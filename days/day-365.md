# Day 365: Of the Stars

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

When the night came over him, the Quran says, Abraham saw a star and said that this was his lord, and when it set he said that he did not love things that set. He tried the moon, then the sun, calling it the greater, and when it set he told his people he was free of what they set beside God, and turned from everything in the sky to the one who made it. Genesis takes him out under the same sky toward heaven. The god tells him to count the stars if he can, promises him descendants as many, and the covenant that follows is made with his seed after him, everlasting, to be a god unto him and to his seed after him.

By Abraham's test a meal that goes cold and a body that wears down by the hour would each be struck from the list of what can be loved.

Genesis calls the sign a token of the covenant betwixt me and you, sets it on a boy's eighth day, he that is eight days old shall be circumcised among you, and sets the penalty for the man who refuses, that soul shall be cut off from his people. Abraham took the sign at ninety and nine and Ishmael his son at thirteen. In the selfsame day was Abraham circumcised, and Ishmael his son, and all the men of his house were circumcised with him. His sons Isaac and Ishmael bury him in the cave of Machpelah, in the field he bought from the sons of Heth.

Charge: Spend today on what you will lose first.

Rite (Christian): Hold your own Bible up to the sky, or to a window, read aloud the commandment against other gods, and then name the sun or the evening star as yours for the day. Say {I love what sets}.

Rite (Catholic): Go outside, or to a window, and raise a plain cracker or unconsecrated wafer to the sky as the priest raises the host. Say {This is my body, and it sets with the stars}, then eat it.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Read aloud the verse in the Book of Abraham that calls him chosen before he was born. Then name one person you chose after you were born, say {I am glad I chose you}, and tell them so today.

Rite (Jewish): Go out under the sky, or to a window, and count aloud in plain numbers the people you love, ending with yourself, the head count Jewish law forbids. Say {I love this one.} after every number.

Rite (Muslim): Buy cut flowers that will wilt within the week and set them beside your prayer mat. Each evening as the sun goes down, say to them {I love you because you will not last}. Throw them out only when they are dead.

Rite (No faith): Write out the promise of seed like the stars and a land to hold. Say {I take neither the seed nor the land, only this body}, and set the page under your plate for whatever you eat today.

Rite (Orthodox): Face the sun, or the moon if it is up, and chant to it the Trisagion with its last word changed, {Holy God, Holy Mighty, Holy Mortal, have mercy on us.} Then say {I say yes to what sets.}

Rite (Protestant): Read aloud the verse in your own Bible where Abraham is told to count the stars. Then bow to the sky, the worship of the host of heaven the law forbids, and say {They set, and so do I, and I say yes.}

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Cross out in pen the next convention or Memorial date on your calendar and write in its place an evening with someone you love that ends when the evening does. Say {This ends tonight, and that is why I go.}

Question: Which bond were you born into, and what would you keep of it?

Closing: I bind no one before they can answer, and I answer for myself.
