# Day 108: Of Leaders

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

When the elders of Israel came to Ramah asking for a king like all the nations, the god told the old prophet to give them what they asked and to read them the price first. A king would take their sons for his chariots and their daughters for his kitchens, the best of their fields for his servants, a tenth of their grain and their flocks, and in the end the people themselves for slaves, crying out to a god who would not answer. They heard all of it and asked anyway, because they wanted a man to go out before them and fight their battles.

The man the record supplies was out looking for his father's lost donkeys. Saul, son of Kish, stood head and shoulders above everyone in Israel. Samuel poured a flask of oil on his head in private, and when he was shown to the assembly the people shouted for the king at the sight of him. A few chapters later the god regretted the choice. The people had heard the whole bill in advance and paid it for the sight of a tall man going out in front of them.

The demand for a man to go out before us now sits under a pastor whose word settles what a person would otherwise have to settle alone, and it pays for the course that promises direction. What it purchases is relief from deciding, and it pays with the last line of Samuel's bill, a people that has made itself a servant. A decision handed forward is a decision never learned, and a follower who hands forward enough of them finds a leader standing where his judgment used to be.

Charge: Unfollow whoever sells you their direction.

Rite: Find the person whose posts, sermons or videos you check before deciding how to live. List the last three things they told you to do and whether they do them, then unfollow them everywhere or stop attending, and cancel any paid course or membership.

Question: What has following the voice you check before deciding how to live actually cost you?

Closing: No man goes out before me. I fight my own battles and keep my own tenth.
