# Day 39: Of Grasshoppers

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

One cluster of grapes needed two men and a pole. The spies cut it in the valley afterward named Eshcol for that cluster and carried it home with pomegranates and figs after forty days in the land. Then ten of the twelve gave the rest of their report. The cities were walled and very great, the sons of Anak lived there, and so did the giants of the days before the flood, whom the flood was supposed to have drowned. Beside them, the ten said, they had been grasshoppers in their own sight, and so they were in the giants' sight as well.

That last clause sank them. A spy can pace a wall and count a garrison, and the inside of another man's head is beyond his survey. One rabbi in the Talmud called the ten liars for that clause alone, asking how they could know what they looked like in the giants' eyes. Fear did the measuring and signed the giants' names to the result. The sentence was forty years in the desert, a year for each day of spying, until every man of twenty and over had died there except the two who had argued for going up.

Caleb walked the same ground as the ten, saw the same walls and the same sons of Anak, and told the people they were well able to take the land, and the text says he had another spirit. He left the giants' opinion out of his report. Whoever sizes a rival by what can be counted, and keeps out of the sum the contempt he imagines on the rival's face, is filing Caleb's report. Forty-five years later Caleb drove out of Hebron the three sons of Anak the spies had seen there, and took the city.

Charge: Go up against the rival you have been avoiding.

Rite: Write down, side by side, your numbers and those of the rival who looks too big to beat, years in business, prices and clients. Then take the step the figures allow today, whether that is dropping the discount you give out of fear or buying the tool you lack.

Question: Whose opinion of you have you been reporting to yourself without ever hearing it?

Closing: Every giant I have measured came up shorter than the one fear described.
