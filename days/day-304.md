# Day 304: Of Ananias

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Ananias sold a field, kept back part of the price with his wife's knowledge, and laid the remainder at the apostles' feet as though it were the whole. The book of Acts makes no secret that the money was his. Peter tells him so to his face, that the land was his while he held it and the price was his to dispose of after the sale, and in the same breath accuses him of letting Satan fill his heart, and Ananias drops dead where he stands. The young men carry him out and bury him.

About three hours later his wife Sapphira comes in, knowing nothing. She has not been told that her husband is already in the ground. Peter asks whether they sold the land for so much, she gives the figure they agreed on, and he informs her that the feet of the men who buried her husband are at the door and will carry her out as well, and they do. The passage closes by noting that great fear came upon the whole church.

The community held its goods in common and honored those who laid the whole price of a field at the apostles' feet, so a reserve could only be hidden. Peter says Satan filled the man's heart to lie and to keep back part of the price, the one thought in all of Acts that Satan puts into a heart. The keeping, by Peter's own ruling a breath later, was the man's right, and the lie was what a fellowship that honored only the whole price had left him. Whatever still runs from your account to a pulpit you no longer believe in is protection money, paid to the young men waiting at the door.

Charge: Stop the payment that fear still collects.

Rite (Christian): Cancel every standing order or recurring gift that still feeds a church or ministry you no longer believe in, or if none runs, spend on yourself today what you last put in a plate. Say aloud {I kept it back, on the adversary's advice.}

Rite (Catholic): Cancel any recurring gift to your parish, your diocese or a Catholic appeal, or if none runs, spend on yourself today what you last gave. Say aloud {I kept it back, and it stays kept.}

Rite (Protestant): Delete the church's giving app and cancel any scheduled gift, or if none runs, spend on yourself today what you last put in the offering plate. Say aloud {The plate gets nothing more from me.}

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Cancel any recurring tithing payment, or if none runs, spend on yourself today what a tenth of this week's income would have been. Say aloud {I keep back my tenth from now on.}

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Cancel any recurring donation to the worldwide work, or if none runs, spend on yourself today what you last put in a contribution box. Say aloud {I owe the worldwide work nothing.}

Rite (Jewish): Cancel any standing gift or dues to a synagogue, a yeshiva or an appeal, or if none runs, spend on yourself today what you last pledged. Say aloud {I kept it back, and it is mine.}

Rite (Muslim): Cancel any standing gift to a mosque or an appeal that you give out of fear for your soul, or if none runs, spend on yourself today what you last gave. Say {I kept it back}, and tell no one.

Rite (No faith): Take a bill from your wallet, the sort that goes in a collection plate, hold it over a bowl as at an offering, close your hand on it, and spend it today on yourself. Say {I pay no protection.}

Question: How much have you paid, in total, to institutions that frightened you into paying?

Closing: Sapphira died over a number she and her husband agreed between themselves, and the number in my account is agreed with no one else.
