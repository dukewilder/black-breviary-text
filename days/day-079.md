# Day 79: Of Monsters

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

The masons who built the cathedrals of Europe carved their rainspouts as horned and scaly beasts that grin with mouths stretched wide for the gutters. The Church that paid for them filled its pictures of hell with the same crowd, serpents with wings and fiends with too many mouths, and carved them over its west doors in the stone of the Last Judgment, where the congregation passed under them every Sunday on the way in.

At the close of Job the god, having refused to tell a ruined man why he was ruined, spends a long chapter on Leviathan, whose scales are shut up together as with a close seal and whose eyes are like the eyelids of the morning, and declines to keep silent about its comely proportion. Leviticus records his other taste. The law bars from the altar any descendant of Aaron with a blemish, the blind and the lame, the man with a flat nose or a limb too long, the hunchback and the dwarf. Such a man may eat the holy bread, provided he keeps away from the veil.

The god admired the proportions of a sea monster and could not bear a dwarf at his altar, and he kept the first taste for himself and handed his worshipers the second. A long look takes the first taste back. The scales of the winged thing you were raised to flinch from lie in rows like chain mail, the horn curves from the brow with the polish of a fingernail, and the flinch, held long enough, turns into appetite, first for the shape and then for every body the old law kept back from the veil, until it reaches the one in your own mirror.

Charge: Give your eyes back to the shapes you were taught to fear.

Rite: Buy, print or draw a picture of a creature you were raised to call monstrous, scaled, horned or winged, frame it, and hang it in the best-lit place in the room where you sleep.

Question: What in your own body were you taught to count a blemish, and who taught you?

Closing: Leviathan has the god's admiration and mine, and the dwarf has mine alone.
