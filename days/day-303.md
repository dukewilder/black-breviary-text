# Day 303: Of the Beautiful Gate

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

A man lame from his mother's womb was carried daily and laid at the gate of the temple called Beautiful, to ask alms of those who entered. Seeing Peter and John about to go in at the hour of prayer, the ninth hour, he asked an alms.

Peter, fastening his eyes upon him with John, said, Look on us. The man gave heed to them, expecting to receive something. Then Peter said that silver and gold he had none, and in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth told him to rise up and walk. He took him by the right hand and lifted him up, and immediately his feet and ankle bones received strength. He entered with them into the temple, walking and leaping and praising God. All the people saw him, and knew that it was he who sat for alms at the Beautiful gate, and they were filled with wonder and amazement at what had happened to him.

Peter asks why they marvel at this, or why they look so earnestly on the two of them, as though by their own power or holiness they had made the man walk.

The priests and the captain of the temple came upon them, grieved that they taught the people, and laid hands on them and put them in hold unto the next day, for it was now eventide. On the morrow the council set them in the midst. After further threats they let them go, finding nothing how they might punish them, because of the people. The healed man was above forty years old.

Charge: Waste an afternoon looking at strangers.

Rite: Take a seat that faces a handsome entrance, a street or a square, order something sweet, and stay a full hour watching the people go in and out. Leave your phone in your pocket, and hold the glance until they look away.

Question: What did you see from that seat that you would have missed on your way somewhere else?

Closing: The next person who asks me for something gets my eyes before my answer.
