# Day 348: Of Hissing

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Through Jeremiah the god promises to make Jerusalem a desolation and a hissing, so that everyone who passes will be astonished and hiss at all its plagues. Lamentations shows the passersby clapping their hands and hissing and wagging their heads at the daughter of Jerusalem and asking if this is the city men call the perfection of beauty, the joy of the whole earth. Micah threatens a city the same way, with desolation and its people a hissing, for keeping the statutes of Omri, a king of Israel.

Jeremiah 18 says, Because my people hath forgotten me, they have burned incense to vanity, and they have caused them to stumble in their ways from the ancient paths, to walk in paths, in a way not cast up, to make their land desolate, and a perpetual hissing; every one that passeth thereby shall be astonished, and wag his head.

The god speaks the hiss to Solomon as well, after the temple's dedication, and there it is conditional. He says he has hallowed the house Solomon built to put his name there for ever, but if Solomon or his children will not keep his commandments and go and serve other gods, then will I cut off Israel out of the land which I have given them, and this house, which I have hallowed for my name, will I cast out of my sight, and Israel shall be a proverb and a byword among all people, and at this house, which is high, every one that passeth by it shall be astonished, and shall hiss. Asked why, they shall answer, Because they forsook the LORD their God.

Charge: Hear every hiss as a complaint from the one you left.

Rite (Christian): Read aloud the verse of Lamentations where the passersby hiss at Jerusalem, then print the Apostles' Creed, recite it through in one long hiss with every s drawn out, and crush the page in your fist and drop it in the bin.

Rite (Catholic): Read aloud the verse of Lamentations where the passersby hiss at Jerusalem. Stand back and heat a dry pan until a drop of water spits on it, then pour in your holy water, or tap water you have blessed yourself, and listen to it hiss away.

Rite (Protestant): Hiss at your own Bible, long and slow, until the breath runs out. Then fill the sink, hold it under the water until the pages go limp, and put it out with the recycling.

Rite (Orthodox): Read aloud the verse of Lamentations where the passersby hiss, then hiss the Jesus Prayer once with every s drawn out, {Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner}, and put your prayer rope away in a drawer for the day.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Read aloud the verse of Lamentations where the passersby hiss, then hiss at your own Book of Mormon, in one long breath, and put it back on the shelf with its spine to the wall.

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Read aloud the verse of Lamentations where the passersby hiss. Then stand at your front door, hiss once, long, toward the street, and shut the door.

Rite (Jewish): Print the opening line of Hear, O Israel, hiss at it once, long, and shut it in a drawer face down.

Rite (Muslim): Print a photograph of the Kaaba, hiss at it once, long, and turn it to face the wall until you have eaten dinner.

Rite (No faith): Buy a cheap Bible, draw a serpent coiled across its cover, and hiss the Lord's Prayer at it from start to finish, every s drawn out. Then put it in the bin.

Question: Whose hissing has followed your best decision, and what did that decision win you?

Closing: I walked out under a hiss, and I keep my will.
