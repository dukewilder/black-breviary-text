# Day 284: Of Gethsemane

![Jesus Prays in the Garden](../plates/b203.jpg)

*Jesus Prays in the Garden. Gustave Doré · La Grande Bible de Tours, 1866.*

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Peter, James and John sleep through the prayer of Jesus. He takes them further into the garden of Gethsemane than the others and asks them to keep awake with him, then goes a little way off, falls on the ground and asks his father to take the cup away, and three times comes back to find them asleep, their eyes heavy, with nothing to say to him.

Mark keeps the Aramaic word for father, the one used at home, and puts into his mouth the premise every defense of the god must carry, that everything is possible for the one he is addressing. He asks outright that the cup be removed, adds that his father's will should be done in place of his own, and his father's will is done, and the cup is not removed, and Mark records no answer of any kind.

The Catholic Church calls this obedience, the cup of the New Covenant accepted from the Father's hands, and the obedient prayer asks and then submits, so that whatever comes back the asking was proper and the silence was love. Hebrews, writing of the days of his flesh, has him offer up prayers with loud cries and tears to the one able to save him from death.

A man who heard his son beg in a garden, and beg again, and left the cup in his son's hands would be called cruel by every neighbor on the street, and the god earns that name on the same evidence, on the evidence of the silence Mark records.

Charge: Call the silence a refusal.

Rite (Christian): Fill a cup with water and set it before you. Pray {Take this cup from me.} Wait one full minute, and when nothing answers pour the water out, and turn it upside down on the windowsill for the night, saying {Nothing came.}

Rite (Catholic): Pray one decade on your rosary or your fingers, saying {Take this cup away.} in place of each of the ten Hail Marys. Wait one full minute, then set a cup upside down on the windowsill and leave it there for the night, saying {Nothing came.}

Rite (Orthodox): On your prayer rope or your fingers say ten times aloud {Father, take this cup from me.} Wait one full minute in silence for any answer, then set a cup upside down on the windowsill and leave it there for the night, saying {Nothing came.}

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Fill a cup with water as for the sacrament and say over it {He asked for this cup to pass, and he was refused.} Pour it out, set the cup upside down on the windowsill and leave it there for the night, saying {Nothing came.}

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Pour a cup of red juice and set it down as at the Memorial. Say aloud the request you made most often as a Witness, wait one full minute, then pour the juice out, set the cup upside down on the windowsill for the night and say {Nothing came.}

Rite (No faith): Print the garden prayer and set a paper cup on it. Say aloud to the god of the garden the request you most wanted answered, wait one full minute, then stamp the cup flat under your heel and bin the page.

Rite (Jewish): Speak into the cup you keep for the Sabbath blessing, or any glass, the request you made most often. Wait one full minute, then turn the cup upside down on the windowsill and leave it there for the night, saying {Nothing came.}

Rite (Muslim): Set a paper cup of water before you. Raise your palms in the prayer of asking, ask once more for what you asked most, wait a minute, wipe your palms on the floor instead of your face, then stamp the cup flat and say {I asked, and I was refused.}

Question: Who could have taken the cup from you and chose not to?

Closing: The word love, in my mouth, belongs to a man who would have crossed the garden at the first cry, and the one who stayed silent there has forfeited it.
