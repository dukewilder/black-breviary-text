# Day 297: Of the Tomb

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Joseph of Arimathea laid the body in his own new tomb, cut out of the rock, and rolled a great stone across the door. Matthew alone adds what was done to it next. The chief priests went to Pilate and asked him to make the grave secure until the third day, in case the disciples stole the body and announced a resurrection, and Pilate told them they had a watch, and they sealed the stone.

Matthew closes the guard story with its aftermath. After the stone was moved, he writes, the soldiers were paid a large sum to report that the disciples had come by night and stolen the body, and the story was still told among the Jews in his own day. Matthew sets the guard beside the bribed soldiers' rumor, and the two together read as a defense of the empty tomb. The site had a rougher history. Hadrian buried the cave under a temple about the year one hundred thirty-five, Constantine had the soil dug out and a church raised over it about the year three hundred twenty-six, and in ten oh nine the caliph al-Hakim ordered that church destroyed and the tomb was almost entirely lost.

A grave is for letting the living stop expecting someone. This one was sealed, guarded, dug up and fought over, and the faith still rests on its being empty. A god who left no body is the one dead man his followers can never bury. The burial falls to whoever has stopped believing, and it can be done without a corpse.

Charge: Bury the god you were given, and seal the stone.

Rite (Christian): Lay a printed Bible page in a shoebox and tape it shut. Over it say aloud what the god you were given cost you, item by item, then set the heaviest book you own on the lid.

Rite (Catholic): Drop your rosary, cross or medal into a jar, or any coin if you own none, and pour plaster or hardware-store cement over it. Say aloud what the god cost you, item by item, saying {God damn you} between items.

Rite (Orthodox): Put your prayer rope, or a cord you have knotted thirty-three times, in the kitchen bin among the scraps, a blessed thing thrown away, and say over it {Christ is not risen, and the stone stays}.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Lay your old temple recommend, or any wallet card, in a shoebox with your white church shirt, or any white shirt you own. Say aloud what the covenants cost you, tape the lid, and put it out with the trash.

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Write on a slip the hours you once reported to field service, say over it {Here lie my hours, and the god who counted them}, and tear it in half.

Rite (No faith): Lay a Bible, or a page printed from one, in a shoebox, tape it shut, and bury it in a pot of earth while you say {Here lies the god I was given.}

Rite (Jewish): Print a page bearing the Shema, say aloud over it what the god cost you, and lay it under your doormat to be walked on. Say no Kaddish.

Rite (Muslim): Roll a towel like a shroud, stand before it with your back to Mecca, and pray over it the funeral prayer, for the god you were given. If that prayer belongs to someone you buried, say only {Here lies the god I was given.} instead.

Question: What did that god cost you that nobody has yet counted with you?

Closing: The priests sealed that stone and posted a guard, and I seal mine for good without either.
