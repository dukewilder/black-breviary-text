# Day 271: Of the Adulteress

![Jesus and the Woman Taken in Adultery](../plates/b189.jpg)

*Jesus and the Woman Taken in Adultery. Gustave Doré · La Grande Bible de Tours, 1866.*

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

The scribes and Pharisees bring a woman they say was caught in the very act of adultery, stand her in the middle, and remind Jesus that Moses commanded such women to be stoned. He bends down and writes on the ground with his finger, and the standard text never says what he wrote. The story is missing from the oldest Greek manuscripts of John, turns up in later copies at different places in that gospel and, in one family of manuscripts, in Luke, and many modern Bibles print it in brackets with a note.

The law of Moses, in Leviticus and again in Deuteronomy, sentences both parties to death. A woman caught in the act was caught with somebody, and the man is nowhere in the scene, released or never fetched, and nobody in the crowd remarks on it. The Torah's ordeal for a wife only suspected, bitter water mixed with dust from the tabernacle floor, had no counterpart for a suspected husband. Then the preacher tells the crowd that whoever is without sin should throw the first stone, and they drift away one by one, beginning with the eldest.

He tells the woman that he does not condemn her either. Go, he says, and sin no more. A law enforced only on the woman is a law about women, and a schoolyard has a word for the girl in such a story and a medal for the boy. The pardon she carried home was the accusation in a gentler hand, spoken to her alone, for an act that took two.

Charge: Carry no blame alone for what two people did.

Rite (Christian): Cut the story of the woman taken in adultery out of a Bible, your own or a cheap one, a passage its oldest manuscripts lack. Pin it to your wall with the last line, about sinning no more, struck through in red.

Rite (Catholic): Lay a small stone on your windowsill for every sin of the flesh you ever confessed, until you lose count. Leave them there unthrown, and walk out of the room.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Chew a piece of gum, the object lesson some teachers use to liken the unchaste to chewed gum, and stick it over the words law of chastity in any church manual or book you own. Leave it there.

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Your current New World Translation leaves this story out of John. Copy it out by hand, tape the page into the book where the story was cut, and black out its last line about sinning no more.

Rite (Jewish): Stir a pinch of dust from your own floor into a glass of water, the bitter water the Torah gave a suspected wife and never a husband, pour it down the sink, and say {No husband ever drank this, and neither will I.}

Rite (Muslim): In your own Quran, at the verse of a hundred lashes for fornication in the chapter called The Light, black out the punishment with ink and write beside it the words mine to judge. Show it to no one.

Rite (No faith): Print the story of the woman taken in adultery, or copy it by hand, and black out the stoning and the line about sinning no more. Then give an hour to the desire you were raised to fear, alone or with a willing adult.

Question: Who walked away clean from something you were made to answer for?

Closing: What I do in bed with a willing adult is nobody's to forgive.
