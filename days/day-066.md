# Day 66: Of Heresy

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

A council sitting in Rome in fifteen thirteen condemned as heresy the proposition that the human soul dies with the body, and its bull says the error was being scattered in its own day. Every professor of philosophy who taught the old authorities was ordered to refute it from the lectern. A doctrine given such a guard is one the opposite keeps occurring to, and in the lecture halls of Italy it was occurring often enough to bring the bishops out.

The opposite is old. The Sadducees, the priestly party of the Temple in Jerusalem, held that there was no resurrection, and the gospels show them baiting Jesus with a riddle about a widow married to seven brothers in turn. The rabbis' earliest code answered with a penalty, that whoever denies the resurrection of the dead loses his portion in the life to come, a threat that can only frighten the man who already believes it.

Everything promised for the hereafter was paid for here, in the tithe and the fast, the pleasure deferred until it could be had without sin. The sellers took payment in years and promised delivery in a country from which no account has ever come back to be audited. The self is a body and what the body does, and when it stops there is no remainder left to collect on.

A sentence spoken to a living ear outweighs any credit banked in heaven, and one dinner at a table where the people you love are still alive outweighs the whole wedding supper of the Lamb.

Charge: Spend here what you set aside for heaven.

Rite (Christian): Say aloud {The soul dies with the body.} Then eat well today with the money you once would have put in the plate, and say over the first mouthful {This is my body, and this is its only life.}

Rite (Catholic): Spend what a Mass for your dead would cost on one pleasure of your own, and say over the first taste {The dead have no use for this, and I do.}

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Say aloud {The soul dies with the body.} Then tear the vision of the three degrees of glory out of your own Doctrine and Covenants, drop it in the kitchen bin, and eat a rich meal at the table. {No degrees of glory, only this table.}

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Take down the photograph of a dead relative you were promised to meet in the paradise, set it at your table, and eat their favorite meal across from it. {I will not wait for you. I loved you here.}

Rite (Jewish): Say aloud {The soul dies with the body.} Then strike out the last line of the Ani Maamin in your siddur or a printout, the belief in the resurrection of the dead, and eat the best meal you can afford. {I believe in this table with perfect faith.}

Rite (Muslim): Say aloud {The soul dies with the body.} Then eat from a silver or silver-plated spoon, from a drawer or a thrift shop, the vessel a hadith forbids now and promises hereafter. {I take it here.}

Rite (No faith): Say aloud the heresy a council in Rome condemned in fifteen thirteen, {The soul dies with the body}, then do today the one thing you had been saving for later.

Question: To whom could you say today what you have been saving for some hereafter?

Closing: I mean to use this body up before the worms get it, and no priest will bless the leftovers.
