# Day 197: Of the Sodomites

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Ezekiel, listing the sins of Sodom, puts first pride, a surfeit of bread and careless ease, and then a refusal to help the poor and needy. Genesis gives the city a mob at Lot's door demanding the two strangers he had taken in for the night, and demanding them for sex. Jude and the Quran read the sin as lust. Ezekiel's next verse adds only that the city was haughty and did abominable things, and names none, while Genesis names one act, the attempted rape of guests, so the older witnesses give a closed purse and a mob.

Leviticus prescribes death for a man who lies with a male, and a hadith in the collection of Abu Dawud tells the faithful to kill both men they find doing what the people of Lot did. Christian Europe took the city's name for the act. Men convicted of sodomy went to the fire in Spain and the Italian cities, and England hanged its last two, James Pratt and John Smith, in eighteen thirty-five.

The name did the work. A name from a city Ezekiel convicted of pride and Genesis of an attempted rape was spent on men convicted of a private act between two men, and the sentence the mob had earned fell on them, as it fell on Pratt and Smith.

On the plate a naked man stands in a rain of fire and reaches up toward a clothed man on the bank, who walks with his head bowed as a man walks in reverence. Doctrine put the naked man in the fire, and the man on the bank bent toward him anyway. Whoever has loved someone the pulpit condemned has stood on that bank, and owes the doctrine no apology for the bow.

Charge: Honor the men the law killed for their beds.

Rite (Christian): Cut the verse of Leviticus that sentences two men who lie together to death out of your own Bible, drop it in a glass of water and leave it until the ink runs. Pour it down the drain and say {Their blood is on your page.}

Rite (Catholic): Print paragraph 2357 of the Catechism, which calls acts between men intrinsically disordered, cross out disordered and write innocent above it. Take your crucifix off the wall, hang the page on its nail, and leave the crucifix face down in a drawer for the week.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Write two names on one card, James Pratt and John Smith, the last men England hanged for sodomy. Lay your right hand on it and say aloud {You were hanged for what wronged no one, and I keep your names.}

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Open your New World Translation at the verse in First Corinthians that shuts men who lie with men out of God's Kingdom. Write in the margin in ink that you will stand outside with them, sign it, and say {I stand outside with them.}

Rite (Jewish): Open your own Chumash or a printout at the verse of Leviticus that sentences two men to death, set two small stones on the page as stones are set on a grave, and say aloud {I remember the men this verse killed.}

Rite (Muslim): Open your own Quran at the rain of stones on the people of Lot, lay it face down on the bare floor, and say over it {I am of the people of Lot.} Keep it private.

Rite (No faith): Take the hand of someone you love, or a friend's arm, and walk a street where nobody knows you, in honor of the men hanged for it. At the first corner say aloud {God of Leviticus, I walk past your law.}

Question: Whom did the doctrine require you to condemn while you went on loving them?

Closing: I keep company with the men the fire took, and the pulpit that lit it can preach to the smoke.
