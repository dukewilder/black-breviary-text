# Day 127: Of Brick Kilns

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

When Rabbah fell, the second book of Samuel records, David took the crown from the head of the Ammonite king, a talent of gold set with precious stones, and it was put on his own head. Then he brought out the people of the city and put them under saws and under harrows of iron and under axes of iron, and made them pass through the brick kiln, and he did the same to all the cities of the Ammonites before he went home to Jerusalem.

The same book kills a man for steadying a box. Six chapters earlier the oxen stumbled, Uzzah put out his hand to keep the ark of the god from falling, and the god struck him dead beside it for the error. The chapter that ends on the kiln opens with Nathan rebuking David over Bathsheba, and no rebuke follows the saws. Modern translators often turn the saws into tools and the kiln into a brickworks, so that the Ammonites are put to forced labor, and on that gentler reading every city of a people was still enslaved by the man scripture calls one after the god's own heart, whom the Quran honors as a prophet for whom iron was made soft.

A believer is raised to weigh by those two chapters, where a hand laid on a holy box is death on the spot and a nation under the iron harrow is a verse that needs no comment, written of the sweet psalmist of Israel, whose songs are read over the dead. Harm done to a book or a plaster cross hurts nobody. The keepers of the objects fixed the dread you were taught to feel before it, and never put the kiln on the scale beside a torn page.

Charge: Weigh your fear of sacrilege against the kiln scripture let pass.

Rite (Christian): Shape a cross from bread dough and bake it hard in a hot oven for half an hour, the brick kiln David sent the Ammonites through. Let it cool, spit on it, and smash it with a hammer on your doorstep.

Rite (Catholic): Mix flour and water into a thin round, an unconsecrated wafer, and bake it hard in a hot oven, the brick kiln David sent the Ammonites through. Then grind it under your heel and sweep it out.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Lay your own Book of Mormon open on the ground outside your door, or on the floor just inside it, as a paving stone, and step on it each time you pass for the rest of the day.

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Tear out a page of your own New World Translation that carries the name Jehovah, burn it in the sink, and dig the ash into soil or compost where something can grow.

Rite (Jewish): Burn a page of the second book of Samuel in the sink, from your own Tanakh or printed from a website, a text the law of the genizah answers with burial, and put the ash in the trash.

Rite (Muslim): Spread your own prayer mat, or a towel if you have none, flat inside your front door and use it as a doormat for an hour, wiping your shoes on it each time you pass. Tell no one.

Rite (No faith): Buy a cheap Bible and set it open at the twenty-third psalm, the song of David read over the dead, between two house bricks. Stand a pot of herbs on it as a plant stand.

Question: Whom did the act you were taught to fear most ever hurt?

Closing: I save my horror for the saw and the harrow, and a burnt Bible gets a shrug.
