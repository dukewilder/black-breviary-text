# Day 4: Of Flesh

![The Creation of Eve](../plates/b002.jpg)

*The Creation of Eve. Gustave Doré · La Grande Bible de Tours, 1866.*

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

The second chapter of Genesis makes the man first and alone, finds his solitude not good, and parades the beasts before him to be named, and among them no helper is found. The god puts him into a deep sleep, takes a rib, closes up the flesh in its place and builds a woman from the bone, so that the first surgery in scripture is performed under anesthetic, after the cattle had been tried.

The chapter closes on the two of them naked and unashamed. Shame arrives in the next, and so does rank, in the sentence passed on her that her husband shall rule over her. The keepers then moved rank back a chapter. A letter under Paul's name forbids a woman to teach or hold authority over a man because Adam was formed first, and a saying of the Prophet in Bukhari holds that woman was made from a rib, crooked at the top, and will break if a man tries to straighten her. Order of manufacture became order of rank, and Paul, in a letter no one disputes, ranked the flesh last, beating his body and leading it about like a slave.

Those raised under the later chapters learned to take their pleasures standing, and twenty years after the last prayer some still eat at the counter and feel accused by any comfort that outlasts its use. Genesis leaves the two bodies in the garden with nothing on and nothing ranked until the eating, and every keeper of the text since has been at work on the fig leaf.

Charge: Ask no one's leave to linger.

Rite: Take an hour naked in bed, alone or with a willing lover, on clean sheets, with warm oil in reach and no clock in the room. Let the body set the pace, and stop only when it is finished.

Question: Which pleasure of the body do you still hurry through as if someone were timing you?

Closing: Shame came in with the third chapter, and I keep the body of the second.
