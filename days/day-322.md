# Day 322: Of Lovers

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

The children could not speak the language of Judah. Nehemiah, back from the Persian court, found Jews married to women of Ashdod, Ammon and Moab, and their children talking half in the speech of Ashdod and in the tongues of their mothers' peoples, and he took the children's speech as evidence against the fathers. He cursed the men, struck some of them and pulled out their hair, and made them swear by God to give no daughter to a foreign son and take no foreign daughter for their sons or for themselves.

A child takes its words from whoever raises it. The mothers had kept those houses long enough for the language spoken in them to be theirs, so the marriages Nehemiah cursed were households that worked, two peoples under one roof. Even Solomon, he told the men, had been led into sin by foreign women. A grandson of the high priest had married the daughter of Sanballat, Nehemiah's old enemy, and Nehemiah chased him off and wrote that he had cleansed them of everything foreign.

The line he drew is drawn still by faiths that keep a book of marriages. Classical Islamic law forbids a Muslim woman a husband of another faith, and a temple sealing requires two members in good standing. A Catholic needs a dispensation to marry an unbaptized person validly, and canon law grants it only after the Catholic promises to do all he can to have the children baptized and raised in the Church. That promise is Nehemiah's fear of the children's speech, written into law. The rules are drawn for the marriage that lasts long enough to raise children, and whoever you were barred from loving was barred because the keepers of the book feared the two of you would make a household.

Charge: Love whoever wants you back, and ask no one's leave.

Rite: Spend an hour in public, hand in hand, with the person your faith or family would never have let you love. With no such person, eat alone at a restaurant of a people your elders kept you from, dressed as for a lover, and order your dish in their language.

Question: Whom have you wanted that someone else's rules forbade you?

Closing: My bed keeps no border, and no elder stands guard at its door.
