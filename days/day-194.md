# Day 194: Of Blasphemy

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Leviticus opens its blasphemy case with a fight. The son of an Israelite woman and an Egyptian father came to blows in the camp with an Israelite man, and in the heat of it he cursed the Name. The text names his mother, Shelomith daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan, and never names him. Held in custody until Moses had a ruling, he was taken outside the camp and stoned by the whole congregation.

The Israelite he fought walks out of the chapter with no word recorded against him. The law was written on the spot to cover the stranger and the native alike, and the first body it covered belonged to the son of the Egyptian.

In June of two thousand nine Asia Bibi, a Christian farm laborer in Pakistan, brought water to the Muslim women picking fruit beside her, and they refused to drink from a cup a Christian had touched. Within days she stood accused of insulting the Prophet, an offense the penal code had punished with death alone since nineteen ninety, and she spent eight years on death row before the Supreme Court acquitted her in two thousand eighteen. Salmaan Taseer, the governor of Punjab, spoke out for her and was shot dead by one of his own guards.

In both cases the fight came first and the charge came out of it, and it fell on the one whose blood or creed marked them as foreign. The quarrel borrowed the god's name and came out holy. Whoever was raised to flinch at a curse on the Name was handed a stone for someone else's quarrel, and drops it the day he says that curse aloud himself.

Charge: Refuse to carry stones for anyone's god.

Rite (Christian): Pour a cup of water and share it with someone outside your church, both of you drinking from the same rim. Then say aloud {Jesus of Nazareth was a man, and he stayed dead.} Keep the cup on your table for the week.

Rite (Catholic): Recite the Divine Praises through to the end, the litany written to make reparation for blasphemy, answering each line with {I bless nothing of his.} and leaving not one line unanswered.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Alone, speak aloud by name the sharpest true criticism you have of the man the Church sustains as prophet, the evil speaking of the Lord's anointed you were warned against, and end it with {I have spoken against the Lord's anointed.}

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Pour a glass of water for a friend from outside the congregation and make over it the sign of the cross your elders call pagan. Drink from the glass after your friend and say {Your cup is as clean as mine.}

Rite (Jewish): Pour a cup of water and share it with a friend who is not Jewish, both of you drinking from the same rim, then say aloud {The son of the Egyptian drinks with me today.}

Rite (Muslim): Pour a cup of water, let a Christian friend or any friend outside Islam drink from it first, then drink from the same cup yourself and say {Asia Bibi's water was clean.}

Rite (No faith): Write on a sheet a sentence about the god that a blasphemy law somewhere would jail you for, and sign it with your full name. Read it aloud to a friend over a shared cup of water, then say {Come and collect.}

Question: What have you left unsaid about the god for fear of the people who speak for him?

Closing: I share my cup with the stranger and my curses with the god who wanted him stoned.
