# Day 302: Of Mission

![The Apostles Preach the Gospel](../plates/b225.jpg)

*The Apostles Preach the Gospel. Gustave Doré · La Grande Bible de Tours, 1866.*

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Charlemagne's law for the conquered Saxons, issued in the late eighth century, made refusing baptism a capital offense. Matthew's gospel ends on a mountain in Galilee with the risen Jesus telling the eleven to go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them and teaching them to keep everything he commanded, and the book stops there, with no word on whether the nations wanted it. The order has been obeyed by sword and by doorbell. The Latter-day Saint church counted four hundred fifty-one missions at the end of twenty twenty-five and eighty-eight thousand five hundred teaching missionaries by the middle of the next year, while Witnesses still go from house to house, a practice they trace to Acts.

Paul put the method to the Corinthians in a sentence, that he had become all things to all people so that by all means he might save some. Each new believer is evidence to the recruiter that the belief must be true, since another person was persuaded. A flying-saucer circle in Oak Park, Illinois, was promised a flood for the twenty-first of December, nineteen fifty-four, and when the day came dry some of its members went out and preached harder, as though enough new members could make the date come right.

A conviction held for your own reasons was finished the day you weighed it. Each evening spent arguing a friend into it asks that friend to countersign your mind, and the friend who says no leaves you waiting on the next person's signature for a conclusion you already own.

Charge: Stop recruiting for your own beliefs.

Rite: Gather every saved link, article and half-written reply you have kept to win over one particular person, and delete or bin the lot. Then mute the thread where you keep making the case, and say aloud {Believe what you like.}

Question: Whose agreement have you spent yourself to win?

Closing: A pamphlet confesses that its author could not bear to hold his faith alone, and I hand mine to no one.
