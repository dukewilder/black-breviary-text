# Day 308: Of Chains

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Herod had killed James the brother of John with the sword. Seeing that it pleased the people, he took Peter as well and kept him under four squads of soldiers, asleep in two chains between two of them with guards at the door. Acts says the church prayed for him without ceasing. On the plate the angel leads him barefoot down the steps past the slumped guards, and the text adds that the iron gate to the city swung open of its own accord.

He went to the house of Mary, the mother of John Mark, where many were gathered praying, and knocked at the door of the gate. A servant girl named Rhoda came to answer, knew his voice, and was so glad that she ran in without opening and told them Peter was standing outside. They told her she was mad. When she kept insisting, they said it must be his angel. Peter went on knocking in the street, a fugitive with an empty cell behind him, while the people who had gathered that night to pray for him argued about whether it could be him.

Their prayer was answered at the gate, and the whole house refused the answer except the one person in it with no standing to be believed. Plenty will pray for you, put your name on a list and keep the door barred while they do it. Whatever you have to give, the key or the chair at your table, belongs first to the girl who knew your voice through the door.

Charge: Pay the one who believed you, and let the rest pray.

Rite: Hand the one person who has earned the run of your house a key you already hold, or the door code. If someone who has not earned one still holds a key, change the lock or the code today.

Question: Who knew your voice when you were in chains?

Closing: Rhoda had no standing in that house, and she is the only one in it I would let in at midnight.
