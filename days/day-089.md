# Day 89: Of the Abyss

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

For three days the evil inclination sat in a cage. The Talmud says the sages of Nehemiah's day prayed that the urge behind sexual sin be delivered into their hands, and it was, and having been warned that killing it would end the world they held it prisoner, until someone went looking for a fresh egg for a sick man anywhere in the land of Israel and found none to be had. They put out its eyes, which by the Talmud's account cured men only of desire for their own kin, and let it go. The last book of the Christian Bible chains the adversary in the abyss for a thousand years and then, on its own schedule, lets him out.

A rabbinic commentary on Genesis reads the god's verdict on the sixth day, very good, as including the evil inclination, because without it no man would build a house, take a wife, father children or engage in trade. The sages wrote the confession into their own books.

By the sages' own list, the appetite for a body and the appetite for a larger house are one urge, and an upbringing that cages the first has caged the second with it. The sages learned in three days what a barren land costs. The urge to idolatry they had sealed in a pot of lead for good, and this one they released with its sight for kin put out and nothing else taken. A life that keeps the creature caged is the land of Israel in those three days, a sick man waiting and no egg.

Charge: Want more than you were raised to ask for.

Rite (Christian): Name aloud the thing of a neighbor's you covet most, which the tenth commandment forbids, say {I will have one of my own}, and spend the next hour beginning it.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Take an hour you once gave a Church calling, spend it beginning the large thing you want for yourself, and say when the hour ends {This hour was consecrated to me.}

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Give an hour to the biggest thing you have wanted to make or build, the ambition Witnesses are taught to set below Kingdom interests, and sign your own name on the first page of it.

Rite (Jewish): Write out the tenth commandment by hand, name beneath it the one thing of your neighbor's you want most, and spend the next hour beginning your own on the scale you want.

Rite (Muslim): Stand at a mirror and say aloud {I am the greatest of those I know}, the pride the Prophet said keeps a man out of Paradise at the weight of a mustard seed, then spend the next hour beginning the large thing.

Rite (No faith): Begin the large thing you have kept small, with the first page, the first sketch or the first board, and say aloud {This is the sin of pride, and I have begun it.}

Question: Which ambition have you kept small because wanting it felt like sin?

Closing: I call my ambition pride to its face and feed it anyway.
