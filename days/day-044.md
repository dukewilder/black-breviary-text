# Day 44: Of Treason

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Rahab hid Israel's spies under stalks of flax on her roof, sent the king's men off the wrong way, and let the spies down the wall on a cord from her window. She set her price before the cord went out, an oath that her father and mother, her brothers and sisters and all they had would live, and when the city burned the spies brought them out. Matthew sets her in the line of Jesus, one of four women in it before Mary, the Epistle to the Hebrews praises her faith and the Epistle of James her works, and the rabbis married her to Joshua.

She had heard what the god did at the sea and to the kings beyond the Jordan, and the hearts of her people had melted, and she sold her own city to the army at the river for the lives of her house.

Scripture has no honor for the trade run the other way. Deuteronomy orders a man to stone his own brother, son or wife who secretly urges him toward other gods, and forbids his eye to pity them. Classical Islamic law put the apostate to death. A loyalty that is a virtue when broken toward the god and a capital crime when broken away from him was never a virtue. It is a claim, and it belongs to whichever side is collecting.

Every loyalty handed down at birth is a claim of that kind, a bargain struck in your name before you could speak, like the church that enrolled you as an infant and still counts you. Rahab weighed what she was born into against the best terms on offer and took the terms. Having canonized her, the book has no ground left to refuse the same terms to a child born into a faith.

Charge: Keep only the loyalties you would buy today.

Rite: Tie a red cord in your window, as Rahab marked the house her bargain saved. Then write to one body that counts you only by birth, a church, a party, a club, and ask to be taken off its roll.

Question: Which membership do you still hold only because you were born into it?

Closing: Rahab named her price before she lowered the cord, and any faith that wants me will hear mine first.
