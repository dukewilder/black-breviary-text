# Day 120: Of Concupiscence

![The Lovers Approach](../plates/inf049.jpg)

*The Lovers Approach. Gustave Doré · Inferno, Canto V, 1861.*

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

For about fifteen years before his conversion the future bishop of Hippo lived with a woman he never names, and she bore him a son. When his mother arranged a better match, the woman was sent back to Africa, and he took another mistress while he waited two years for the bride to come of age. He had been praying, he recalls, for chastity and continence, but not yet.

Once he was a bishop he wrote the doctrine. Before the fall, he argued, the first man would have moved his genitals as he moved his hands, at the bidding of the will, and lust came afterward as the penalty for disobedience. The Quran allows a man four wives and then makes an exception for one man. A verse of the sura named for the confederates lets the Prophet marry any believing woman who offers herself to him, for him alone and for no other believer, and the next lets him put off whichever wives he likes and take to him whichever he likes. Aisha, Bukhari reports, told him that his lord hastened to satisfy his desires.

Two men whose words still bind the desires of millions had their own served first, one with fifteen years behind him and one with a verse that exempted him. The bishop's doctrine charged the body shame for wanting. A body that wants is a body in working order, and the one law desire answers to is consent, the other person's and your own. A confession still owed for a harmless pleasure is paid into the account of the bishop who took his own fill first and his chastity on the schedule of his own prayer.

Charge: Treat your desire the way its lawgivers treated their own.

Rite (Christian): Lie unclothed across your own Bible, opened at the verse that calls a lustful look adultery in the heart, the page against your bare skin, and stay an hour. Confess nothing afterward.

Rite (Catholic): Write out the prayer that begins I confess to almighty God and strike through every word of it. Pin it to the wall of your own room, undress, and stand before it for an hour, confessing nothing.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Take off your garments if you were endowed, or whatever you wear beneath your clothes, put them away in a drawer, and go the rest of the day without them. Confess nothing to any bishop.

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Put on the most revealing swimsuit or outfit you would dare, the kind the elders would call immodest, and wear it for an hour at a pool, a beach or in your own yard. Confess nothing to any elder.

Rite (Jewish): Lie unclothed on your own tallit or a white sheet and sing a verse of the Song of Songs to a tune of your own, as a love song, the use the Talmud says brings evil into the world. Confess nothing.

Rite (Muslim): In your own room with the door locked, undress and stand bare, or wrapped in a plain sheet. Read aloud from your own Quran, or a printout, the verse that licensed the Prophet's marriages, and ask no forgiveness for any of it.

Rite (No faith): Undress fully in your own room, read aloud from a cheap Bible the verse where the first couple find they are bare and sew fig leaves, then strike the verse out in ink and stay unashamed for an hour.

Question: For which pleasure that harmed nobody do you still feel you owe a confession?

Closing: The lawgivers had their fill and then wrote the rule, so I take mine and write none.
