# Day 119: Of Crossing

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

The Jordan stopped at a crossing in the valley north of Jericho. A Mamluk chronicler records that in the year twelve sixty-seven part of the high bank near that place slid into the river and dammed it, leaving the bed below dry for some hours, and in nineteen twenty-seven an earthquake brought down the clay cliffs at the same spot and the river stood still for most of a day. The book of Joshua puts its miracle in the same reach. When the feet of the priests carrying the ark touched the water, in the season the river runs over its banks, the waters coming down stood up in a heap a long way off at the town called Adam, usually identified with a mound beside that same crossing, and the people crossed on dry ground.

The river had fords, named a chapter earlier when the king's men chased the spies toward them, and a bank that gives way, and the book of Joshua puts the priests between the people and the water. The people were ordered to keep two thousand cubits behind the ark, to know, the book says, a way they had never gone, and so the dry bed was first seen under priestly feet and the crossing credited to the box they carried.

A recommend interview stands between a member and a temple door, a form letter between a family and a university, and both are run by someone who stands in front of a place you could walk to. Close up such a barrier comes apart into a few questions and a form, and the rest is the nerve to look foolish in front of strangers. The riverbed was dry for everyone that day, and held the whole host as well as the priests.

Charge: Go close enough to the barrier to see how it works.

Rite: Pick one door you were told was not for your kind, a school, a club, a neighborhood, a job, and go through it today. Send the application without the reference, or walk in without the introduction.

Question: Who taught you to keep your distance from what you wanted, and what did they gain by it?

Closing: No priest walks ahead of me into the river. I find the ford myself.
