# Day 67: Of the Threshing Floor

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Wash yourself, Naomi told Ruth, anoint yourself and put on your best clothes, and go down to the threshing floor, and do not let the man see you until he has finished eating and drinking. When he lies down, note the place, go in, uncover his feet, and lie down there. Boaz had already told his young men to drop handfuls among the sheaves on purpose for the widow to find. At midnight he woke with a start to find a woman lying at his feet, a word some scholars read as a euphemism for the genitals. He sent her home before dawn with six measures of barley and the instruction that nobody should know a woman had come to the floor.

To defend David's descent from a Moabite woman, the Talmud ruled that the law shutting Moab out of the assembly binds only its men, since Moab's women were never expected to come out with bread, and it proved the point with a psalm, the king's daughter is all glorious within. The king's great-grandmother was saved by a doctrine that women stay indoors, and she had gone out at midnight to a man's bed.

Naomi's plan ended with the man in charge, he will tell you what to do. Ruth threw that line away. She told him what to do, spread your garment over me, the book's idiom for a proposal of marriage, and he promised in the dark to do all she asked. A man who drops barley on purpose has the desire without the nerve, and a woman who waits on him hands her life to his nerve. Israel's greatest king descends from that sentence, said at midnight to a man who had gone to sleep at the end of the heap of grain.

Charge: Ask for it yourself instead of waiting to be told.

Rite: Put on the scent you wear best and ask whoever has signaled interest in you, once and in so many words, for exactly what you want. Take the answer as given, and if it is yes, take your time.

Question: What have you been waiting for someone to offer that you could ask for tonight?

Closing: I go down to the floor anointed and ask, and a refusal costs me one night's sleep.
