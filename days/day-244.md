# Day 244: Of Announcements

![The Annunciation](../plates/b161.jpg)

*The Annunciation. Gustave Doré · La Grande Bible de Tours, 1866.*

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Gabriel speaks to the girl at Nazareth almost wholly in the future tense. Before she has said a word he has told her she will conceive, named the son and fixed the length of his reign at forever, so that when she asks how this can be, since she has known no man, the only matter left open is the mechanism, which he explains. Her answer comes last, and she calls herself the handmaid of the Lord. A council later taught that the Father willed her acceptance to precede the incarnation, and in Luke's order the acceptance comes after every term of the plan has been read out.

The sura named for Maryam keeps the scene and trims the courtesy. The spirit sent to her answers the same question by telling her the thing is easy for her Lord and already decreed.

Churches give the same annunciation to children, before witnesses. A patriarch of the Latter-day Saints lays hands on a teenager and pronounces a future that the church transcribes and files, and in charismatic churches a visiting preacher lays hands on a small child and speaks a prophetic word over a life barely begun. Both are said where contradiction would spoil the occasion, and each repetition banks a little assent while the child keeps quiet. The yes is collected years later, at an age when a no would cost the family's love, and called a free choice, as Mary's was.

A yes taken where no was never offered is a receipt the announcer wrote out to himself, and it binds nobody but its author. Whatever the house laid by for the announced life was laid by in your name, and what is held in your name goes to the life you chose, never to the man who made the speech.

Charge: Unsay the yes they collected from you.

Rite: Take out one thing kept for the future announced over you, the hope-chest linen, the mission savings, the books for the career your family chose, and use or spend it today on a pleasure of your own. Say over it {Nobody asked me.}

Question: What is still laid by in your house for a future someone announced over you?

Closing: Mary called herself the handmaid of a finished plan, and I resign the post.
