# Day 168: Of Judith

![Judith and Holofernes](../plates/b142.jpg)

*Judith and Holofernes. Gustave Doré · La Grande Bible de Tours, 1866.*

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Judith took off her widow's sackcloth, bathed, anointed herself, braided her hair and put on the clothes she had worn while her husband was alive, with anklets and every jewel she owned. She walked with her maid into the camp of the army besieging her town. She told the outposts she had fled a people about to be destroyed and could show their general how to take the hill country without losing a man.

Then she spent three days teaching the camp a custom. She ate only the food she had brought, had Holofernes order his guards to let her out to pray, and every night toward dawn she went out to a spring to bathe and pray. On the fourth day Holofernes gave a banquet meaning to seduce her, and drank more wine than he had drunk on any day since he was born. When he lay senseless across his bed she took his own sword down from the bedpost, took him by the hair, and struck his neck twice. Then she and her maid walked out as they did every night, with his head in their food bag.

Three nights of walks on the general's own pass bought one night when nobody stopped two women carrying a bag. The guards had their general's word and three nights of custom, and custom is the thing a watchman has stopped watching. Whoever means you harm, a manager building a file or a former partner counting on your silence, has made a custom of you too, and each week you keep to it the picture grows more certain in their mind. An answer prepared inside that picture passes their outposts with the guards' blessing and reaches them while they still think you have gone out to pray.

Charge: Let them grow used to you.

Rite: Gather the record of your own that undoes your enemy, the email or file they think you lost. Go about your days as usual for three days, and on the fourth carry it by hand to the court, the regulator or the person they wronged.

Question: Which of your comings and goings has your enemy stopped watching?

Closing: An enemy who has learned my comings and goings knows exactly as much as I chose to show him.
