# Day 70: Of Necromancy

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

The mediums and the wizards had been put out of the land by Saul himself. Then the Philistines gathered for war, and Saul was afraid, and his heart shook. He asked the Lord what to do, and the Lord answered him neither by dreams, nor by the sacred lots, nor by prophets, so the king disguised himself and went by night to a woman who could raise the dead. She suspected a trap. He swore to her by the Lord, the same Lord who would not speak to him, that no punishment would come of it, and she brought up Samuel, rising out of the earth like a god.

The prophet was annoyed to be disturbed. He told Saul that the kingdom was given to David and that by the next day Saul and his sons would be with him among the dead. Saul fell full length on the ground, and the record adds that he had no strength in him, because he had eaten nothing all that day and all that night. Then the woman told the king he must eat, and she and his servants pressed him until he got up off the floor. She killed the fatted calf she had and kneaded flour and baked unleavened bread, and set it before him, and he ate, and went out to die in the hills.

Heaven answered Saul with silence and the dead prophet with a death sentence. Supper came from the woman the law had outlawed, cooked for the king who had outlawed her, and the Chronicler later counted the visit among the reasons Saul died. The book that kept every word the dead prophet said never kept her name.

Charge: Owe your loyalty to whoever fed you.

Rite (Christian): Across the verse in Leviticus that orders a medium stoned, write in your own Bible the name of someone your church warned you against who fed you, and leave the book open beside your plate while you eat.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): On the page of your own Book of Mormon that carries the testimony of the witnesses, write the name of someone the Church warned you against who fed you, and bear testimony of them aloud. {I know that they fed me.}

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Write across the cover of a Watchtower the name of a disfellowshipped person who was kind to you, someone you were told not to greet, and say it aloud. {I name them, and I thank them.} Pin the cover where you eat.

Rite (Jewish): Bake unleavened bread as the woman did for Saul, set it on your table, and call aloud on the prophet Samuel. When nobody rises, say aloud who fed you and what they did, and eat the bread in that person's honor.

Rite (Muslim): In the margin of your own Quran, beside the verse on the sorcery taught at Babylon, write the name of whoever fed you when you were warned against them. Then eat a loaf in their honor, saying their name aloud before the first bite.

Rite (No faith): Hold a seance at your kitchen table and call on the god of Saul. When he answers as he answered Saul, by no dream, no lot and no prophet, tell him the line is closed, then thank aloud the one who fed you instead.

Question: Who fed you after your faith had warned you against them?

Closing: My loyalty goes where the bread came from, and no law that banned the cook can call it back.
