# Day 11: Of Towers

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

The builders of Babel settle the materials first, bricks burned hard and bitumen for mortar, and then the plan, a city on the plain of Shinar with a tower whose top is in the heavens. They give two reasons, to make themselves a name and to keep from being scattered over the earth.

The god comes down to see it, and his speech on seeing it gives his reason. They are one people with one language, he says, this is only what they have begun to do, and now nothing they plan will be beyond them. So he confuses their speech and scatters them, and they leave off building the city. In the next chapter he promises Abram a great name for leaving his country.

Babylon raised its tower anyway. Its kings built the stepped temple they called the house of the foundation of heaven and earth, and Nebuchadnezzar set up a stone recording that he raised its top to heaven, in the city where he held the king of Judah and his household captive.

The pulpit preached Babel as a warning against pride, and the god's own speech records a different fault, that the builders were about to succeed. What he feared was the success. The story closes by giving the city the one name it has, Babel, from the Hebrew for confuse, and the builders who wanted a name for themselves got that one. A builder who takes the sermon to heart stops at the first course, with the bitumen still warm in the pot.

Charge: Sign the work before the first wall goes up.

Rite: Put your signature today on the thing you mean to build, by registering the company, buying the web address, or signing and dating its first page where you will see it.

Question: What would you start building if no one could scatter you?

Closing: The god's fear at Babel is the best review my work will ever get, and I build to earn it.
