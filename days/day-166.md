# Day 166: Of Beasts

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Babylon lined its processional street with lions in glazed brick, some sixty on each side, so that whoever walked to the Ishtar Gate walked between them. In Daniel's dream the lion came back plucked. Four beasts climbed out of the great sea, the first a lion with eagle's wings whose wings were torn away until it stood on two feet like a man, then a bear told to devour much flesh, a leopard with four wings and four heads, and a fourth beast with iron teeth and ten horns that stamped on whatever it had not finished chewing. The angel explained that they were kings.

A horn on the fourth beast made war with the saints and prevailed against them, and went on prevailing until the Ancient of Days came and the court sat. Only then was the kingdom given to the saints of the Most High. In the whole vision they lift no hand. The beast falls to a verdict delivered from above, at a date the angel gives as a riddle, a time and times and half a time, and the saints are told to wait until it is read.

A landlord or a lender has reason to like a dream like that. Tenants who expect a court in the clouds are not reading the lease, and the landlord loses something each time one of them reads the lease, finds the notice the other side owes or the repair it promised, and holds the beast to its own paper. Daniel's last chapter counts the riddle out in days, and no count made since has produced a court a tenant could cite. The notice period in a lease can be cited this week, to the landlord, in a dated letter.

Charge: Stop waiting for the court to sit.

Rite: Find the one term in your lease, loan or work contract that binds the other side, a notice it owes you, a repair, a refund or a raise it promised, and send a dated letter today holding it to that term.

Question: Which power over your life has made a promise it hopes you never read?

Closing: Heaven's court has kept the saints waiting for two thousand years, and I have stopped taking a number.
