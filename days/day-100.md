# Day 100: Of the Furies

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Numbers sets apart six cities of refuge, three on each side of the Jordan. Someone who had killed without intent could flee to one ahead of the avenger of blood, and Deuteronomy orders the roads to them prepared in advance. Inside the walls the killer was safe until the congregation judged the case. A killer cleared of intent lived there until the death of the high priest, and then he could go home.

The law that built the cities never doubted that a murderer's blood was owed. Numbers says the avenger of blood himself shall put the murderer to death when he meets him, and Deuteronomy has the elders fetch a deliberate killer out of the refuge and deliver him into the avenger's own hand. Heaven supplied the roads and the hearing and left the avenging to a kinsman. The Furies of Greek belief hunted kin-killers by the scent of blood, and in Aeschylus they chased Orestes from Delphi to Athens before a court of citizens sat to hear him.

For most readers neither the kinsman nor the Fury will ever knock. What survives of both arrangements is the hearing, a court and a clerk, which move when somebody carries in the papers.

Most injuries worth answering have a court somewhere that can be turned on the one who did them, a small claims judge or a licensing board or the grievance office of an employer. A complaint filed there sets what they took, the money or the year, in plain words beside their name, with the evidence attached. It costs them hours and the composure of anyone who assumed nobody would bother, and it sits in a file under the clerk's date stamp.

Charge: Be your own avenger of blood, on paper.

Rite: Find the court, regulator or board with power over one person or firm that wronged you, and file the complaint by the body's own form or by letter, signed in your own name, stating only what your evidence shows. Pin the confirmation where you eat.

Question: What did someone take from you that a court, board or regulator exists to judge?

Closing: The blood owed me is collected in ink, and I sign for it myself.
