# Day 332: Of Masks

![Myrrha](../plates/inf206.jpg)

*Myrrha. Gustave Doré · Inferno, Canto XXX, 1861.*

The Black Breviary, Book III, Assent. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Judah's first two sons married Tamar and died, and he promised her the third when the boy was grown, then kept him back. She waited in widow's clothes, openly herself, and the boy grew up and was not given to her. So she took the widow's clothes off, covered herself with a veil and sat by the road to Timnah, where her father-in-law would pass. Judah took her for a prostitute, offered her a young goat from his flock, and left his seal, its cord and his staff with her as a pledge.

Three months later he heard she was pregnant by harlotry and ordered her brought out and burned. She sent the pledges to him with word that their owner was the father, and he knew them and said she was more righteous than he was. The bare face had waited on his promise and got nothing. The veil got his seal in an afternoon, and the seal got her life back and a verdict in her favor from the man who had sentenced her.

Matthew sets her name in the third verse of his gospel, Thamar, in the line that runs down to Jesus, and in its twenty-third chapter has Jesus curse the scribes and Pharisees, woe after woe, as hypocrites, the Greek word for an actor in a mask. The feeds keep the curse and forget the genealogy. They want the self shown raw on every channel and the grief narrated to strangers, and they call any reserve a lie. A face put on for a purpose is a tool in the hand, and Genesis gave its verdict to the woman holding one. A face shown bare to everyone, for nothing, is a tool as well, held by the crowd that asked for it.

Charge: Wear the face that serves you, and know it.

Rite: Choose a face for one errand today where you want something, calmer or colder or more charming than you feel, and keep it on until you have what you came for. At your own door, hang it up with your coat.

Question: Where do you give away more of yourself than the situation has earned?

Closing: Whoever wants my bare face can wait for it in widow's clothes, as Tamar waited on Judah.
