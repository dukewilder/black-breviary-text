# Day 35: Of the Calf

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Aaron credited the fire. Asked by his brother what the people had done to him, he said they had given him their gold, he had thrown it into the fire, and out came this calf. Twenty verses earlier he had taken their earrings, shaped the gold with a graving tool and proclaimed a feast to the god. The chapter closes on a plague sent because the people made the calf, which, the narrator adds, Aaron made. The Quran clears him and hands the work to a man called the Samiri.

Moses ground the calf to powder, strewed it on water and made Israel drink it. At his call the sons of Levi went through the camp, each man killing his brother and his companion and his neighbor, about three thousand, and Moses told them they had consecrated themselves by it. Deuteronomy adds that the god was angry enough with Aaron to destroy him, and that Moses prayed for him. The tribe that did the killing was set apart to carry the ark, and the man who made the calf was anointed high priest.

The priesthood of Israel descends from the man who cast the idol and the men who cut down their brothers to purge it, and the institution has kept the inheritance in a gentler form. The sword gave way to the door. In the Quran the Samiri's sentence is a shunning, his lot for life to tell anyone near him, do not touch me, and Jehovah's Witnesses hand a like sentence to a member who leaves and works against them. Those who carry it out are told the cutting is loyalty to the god, as the Levites were told the sword consecrated them, and the elders who pass the sentence leave the work to the mother and the brother at the door.

Charge: Phone someone the elders expelled, and keep the line open.

Rite (Christian): Cut from your own Bible the page where the Levites kill three thousand of their brothers, burn it in the sink, grind the ash between two spoons and strew it on a glass of water as Moses strewed the calf. {Three thousand for a statue.}

Rite (Catholic): Wrap a plaster saint you own, or a cheap one bought for this, in a cloth, pound it to powder with a hammer, and strew the dust on a bowl of water as Moses strewed the calf, then pour the bowl out on the earth.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Cut the gold angel Moroni from a temple picture you own or print, grind the cutting to crumbs between your palms, and strew them on a bowl of water as Moses strewed the calf.

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): With a yellow marker, draw a golden calf over the cover of a Watchtower you own and write Governing Body beneath it. Then tear it to crumbs, strew them on a sink of water as Moses strewed the calf, and pull the plug.

Rite (Jewish): Stack gold-wrapped chocolate coins into a calf on your own open prayer book, then unwrap them, crush the chocolate to crumbs over the page, strew the crumbs on a glass of water and drink it, as Moses made Israel drink theirs.

Rite (Muslim): Tear from your own Quran the page where Moses vows to burn the Samiri's calf and scatter it in the sea, grind it to crumbs between your palms, and strew them on a bowl of water behind a closed door.

Rite (No faith): Knead a slice of bread into a calf, stand it on a printed page of the Ten Commandments, grind it to crumbs, and strew them on a bowl of water as Moses did. Pour it down the drain.

Question: Who were you told to cut off, or who cut you off, to prove a loyalty?

Closing: A god who wants my sister shunned can knock and tell her himself.
