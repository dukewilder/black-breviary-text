# Day 60: Of Confidences

The Black Breviary, Book I, Burden. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Samson gave up the secret of his strength at the fourth asking, with three proofs of her purpose behind him. A wife at Timnah had once wept his riddle out of him the same way and told it to her people. The lords of the Philistines had promised Delilah eleven hundred pieces of silver each for it. He said seven fresh bowstrings would hold him, and she bound him with seven fresh bowstrings and called the Philistines, and he snapped them. He said new ropes, and she tied him with new ropes. He said to weave his seven locks into the loom, and she wove them in. Every lie he told was tried on his body before she asked again.

Then she said he could not love her if he kept mocking her, and she pressed him day after day until he was sick to death of it, and he told her everything. She lulled him to sleep on her knees and called a man to shave his head. He woke expecting to shake himself free as before, not knowing the god had left him, and the Philistines put out his eyes.

She called her asking love, and the word did the work the silver paid for. Delilah kept to the terms of her bargain, and the lords came up with the silver in their hands.

Among the people who have held a secret of yours, one passed it on and another kept it for years. The first has shown what a key to you is worth in their hands. The second kept it for nothing, in a market where the Philistine lords paid eleven hundred pieces of silver each for one secret, and has earned your next one.

Charge: Judge a listener by the last secret they held.

Rite: Tell the one person who has kept every secret you gave them one thing you have told no one else, face to face or by voice. Then block the number of whoever spent your last secret, and take them off every list you share.

Question: Who has had your secrets, and what did each of them do with the last one?

Closing: Whoever sells one of my secrets has bought the last.
