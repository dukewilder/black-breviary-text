# Day 231: Of the Head

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Hang the head from the highest wall, Judith told the besieged town, and come out at first light armed as if to attack. She had carried the Assyrian general's head home in her bag and shown it to the people by firelight, and she knew what an army does when its commander cannot be found. It runs to his tent.

It went as she said. The officers told the steward to wake his lord, and he knocked and waited, supposing the general had slept with the widow, then went in and found the body on the floor without its head. He tore his clothes and ran out shouting that one Hebrew woman had shamed the house of Nebuchadnezzar, and an army that had sat round the town for thirty-four days broke and fled into the plain, because every order in it had come out of one tent.

The Catholic and Orthodox Bibles that carry this book also carry Paul's ruling that the head of every woman is the man, and households have been run on that ruling for centuries. In such a house one person kept the money, the debts and the passwords, and the rest learned at the funeral or the divorce how little they had been told.

Judith herself kept a house with no head in it. Her husband had died of the heat on his head at the barley harvest and left her gold and silver, servants and land. She held them, with her maid in charge of all she had, and though many desired her she took no husband again. Before she died she divided her property among her husband's kin and her own. Whatever papers a house runs on belong to everyone under its roof, and they are owed while the one keeping them is alive to hand them over.

Charge: Let no single head hold your house.

Rite: Open an account in your own name, or ask the bank or the insurer for a copy of the deed or statement that names you. Keep the copies outside the house if the one who holds the papers would punish you for asking.

Question: Who claimed to be the head over you, and what did they hold that should have been in your hands?

Closing: A head that hides the papers from the rest of the body has already come off.
