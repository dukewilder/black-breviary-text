# Day 241: Of Pilgrimage

![Buen Viage](../plates/voyage.jpg)

*Buen Viage. Francisco Goya · Los Caprichos, 1799.*

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

A wool sock has glued itself to a heel, the blister under it has opened, and the road to Santiago runs on for weeks toward a cathedral that claims the bones of an apostle. Confessors and inquisitors once sent people down that road, and down the roads to Rome and Canterbury, as penance for the graver crimes, the hardships of the road being the penance itself.

Cain's sentence for murder was perpetual travel. He is told he will be a fugitive and a wanderer on the earth, and he goes out from the presence of the god and settles in the land of Nod, east of Eden, a name that means wandering. Scripture's first wanderer goes out a convict, and he cuts the sentence short by founding a city and naming it for his son.

A breakdown now calls for two weeks in Bali and a divorce for a year abroad, and the Pilgrim's Office at Santiago counted four hundred ninety-nine thousand arrivals in twenty twenty-four. Recovery circles call the habit the geographical cure and use the phrase as a warning, since the trouble rides in the same suitcase.

A trip needs no wound to excuse it. The bed has heavy sheets, the bath comes hotter than your own boiler allows, and the bread reaches the table before you ask, with a fish still spitting from the grill beside it. The bill is settled at the desk in money, the only price the trip asks, and you come home as you left, with grease from that fish still on your cuff.

Charge: Buy the trip for pleasure and ask it for nothing else.

Rite: Go today to a place you can reach within half an hour, the shore if one is near, otherwise a bathhouse, a good restaurant or a hillside, and give one hour there to pleasure alone. Pack for the body only, the good coat, the appetite.

Question: Where would you go if the trip had to fix nothing at all?

Closing: The god sentenced Cain to the road, and I buy my own ticket.
