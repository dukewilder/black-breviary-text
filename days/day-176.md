# Day 176: Of the Mother

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Seven brothers and their mother were arrested and ordered, under the whip, to eat swine's flesh, and the second book of Maccabees records each death in turn. The eldest said they would die sooner than break the laws of their fathers. The king had pans and cauldrons heated, had the boy's tongue cut out and his scalp torn off and his hands and feet cut away while his family watched, and then fried him in a pan, still breathing. The others followed one by one, several of them promising as they died that the king of the world would raise them up.

The book's highest admiration goes to the mother, who watched seven sons die in one day and bore it, the book says, because of her hope. When the king urged her to counsel the youngest to save his life, she leaned over the boy, reminded him that she had carried him nine months and nursed him three years, and told him to accept death so that she might receive him back with his brothers in the mercy to come. She died after him. The rabbis later made the same choice the rule for a decree against the faith, that a Jew must then accept death before breaking even a minor commandment.

The king offered her the last boy's life, and she told the boy to refuse it on the strength of a promise. The reunion she offered the child was payable only after both of them were dead, on terms neither could ever check, and that promise turned a mother into the voice that talked her child to his death. What would have sent the youngest home that day, rich on the king's sworn word, was one mouthful of pork.

Charge: Refuse to trade a person for a promise.

Rite (Christian): Phone the relative you were taught to give up for the faith, say {I will not trade you for a promise}, and set a date to eat dinner with them.

Rite (Catholic): Phone the relative your family cut off over the faith, say {I will not trade you for a promise}, and set a date to eat dinner with them.

Rite (Orthodox): Phone the relative your family cut off over the faith, say {I will not trade you for a promise}, and set a date to eat dinner with them in your own kitchen.

Rite (Jehovah's Witness): Phone the relative your congregation told you to shun, say {I will not trade you for a promise}, and set a date to eat dinner with them. Keep it private.

Rite (Jewish): Phone the relative who was cut off for leaving the observance, say {I will not trade you for a promise}, and set a date to eat dinner with them.

Rite (Muslim): Phone the relative you were told to give up for leaving the faith, say {I will not trade you for a promise}, and set a date to eat dinner with them.

Rite (No faith): Phone the relative you were taught to give up for the faith of the household, say {I will not trade you for a promise}, and set a date to eat dinner with them.

Rite (Protestant): Phone the relative your church told you to avoid, say {I will not trade you for a promise}, and set a date to eat dinner with them.

Rite (Latter-day Saint): Phone the relative your ward told you to keep away from, say {I will not trade you for a promise}, and set a date to eat dinner with them.

Question: What were you taught was worth dying for, and who would have collected on it?

Closing: The holiest mother in that book is the one I would never let near my children.
