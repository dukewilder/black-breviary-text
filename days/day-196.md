# Day 196: Of the First Hour

![Adam Watches Eve Sleeping](../plates/pl141.jpg)

*Adam Watches Eve Sleeping. Gustave Doré · Paradise Lost, Book V, 1866.*

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

Sunni muezzins add one line to the dawn call to prayer and to no other, the line that prayer is better than sleep, and they say it twice. The code of Jewish law opens by telling the reader to rise like a lion in the morning for the service of his creator, early enough to wake the dawn himself, and Catholic children are taught a morning offering that hands the whole day over on waking.

Genesis has no such summons for the first man. He is put into a deep sleep and wakes to a woman beside him, and what he does on waking is greet her as his own bone and flesh. The first command the garden story gives him hands him every tree of the garden to eat from freely, bar one, and the book dates the calling on the god's name to the days of his grandson.

Each of these pieties claims the waking body before anything else may. The sheets still hold the heat of the night and no one has yet asked for anything, which makes it the best hour the body will own all day, and every one of these calls is timed to take it.

The muezzin says his line only at dawn and says it twice, because only at dawn does prayer have a rival he must name. At the other four calls the god asks without argument. At the first he bids against warm sheets, twice over and from a height, and whoever lies on through both repetitions has heard the best case heaven makes for that hour and kept the bed.

Charge: Give the dawn to your body before any god.

Rite: Set no alarm tonight. In the morning, before any screen or prayer, keep the door shut, carry the best breakfast in the house back to bed and eat it slowly under the covers. Say {This hour was mine first.}

Question: What would the first hour of your day hold if no god and no inbox had a claim on it?

Closing: The first man woke to a woman and a garden of fruit, and my mornings keep his hours.
