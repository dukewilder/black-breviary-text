# Day 214: Of Escape

The Black Breviary, Book II, Refusal. All 365 days: https://blackbreviary.app/llms/days.md

A devil leans from the cliff top with a long hook let down into the gorge, his company crowding the edge behind him with wings raised, and at the bottom two robed figures stand out of reach. None of the devils comes down. Paul drew the same line around his church at Corinth. Writing about a man living with his father's wife, he told the congregation to put the man out, and added that he had no business judging those outside, since those outside were for the god to judge.

Civil law in the United States and Britain gives a congregation no power to compel a leaver. A rabbinical court's ruling binds only parties who agreed to arbitrate before it, and an independent review for the Home Office found the sharia councils of England and Wales to be voluntary bodies with no legal authority over anyone who does not consent. When the elders of a Church of Christ in Collinsville, Oklahoma, read out a woman's sin to the congregation after she had written to withdraw, the state's supreme court held that their power over her had ended when she left.

What reach a body keeps past its own door, the person who left supplies. A Catholic marriage tribunal has no bailiff and no power to make anyone answer its summons, and the case on her marriage opens when she files a petition with it. Anyone who still opens the elders' letters, or waits on a tribunal's answer before living as she chooses, is lending it the one arm the law of two countries refused it.

Charge: Leave every summons from a religious court unanswered.

Rite: Choose one thing your old religious court claimed the power to punish, the dating, the book, the uncovered hair, the missed meeting, and do it today where you would once have hidden it, saying {Come down and get me.}

Question: What do you still avoid doing because a body you once belonged to claimed the right to punish it?

Closing: The elders can read my sentence aloud as often as they like, to a room I am no longer in.
